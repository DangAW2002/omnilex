## Easy explanation
Think of a highway upgraded from two bumpy lanes to a multi-level expressway with dedicated express corridors for ambulances, bullet trains for commuters, and side tracks for thousands of delivery bikes.

## Details
Standardized internationally by ITU as IMT-2020 and specified by 3GPP, 5G introduces New Radio (NR) across sub-6 GHz and millimeter-wave frequencies. The architecture rests on three operational pillars: Enhanced Mobile Broadband (eMBB) achieving multi-gigabit throughput, Ultra-Reliable Low-Latency Communications (URLLC) providing sub-millisecond round trips, and Massive Machine-Type Communications (mMTC) supporting up to one million endpoints per square kilometer. Network slicing partitions physical infrastructure into distinct virtual pipelines tailored to security, latency, or throughput requirements.

## Technical definition
The standardized fifth-generation cellular network architecture incorporating flexible radio numerology, service-based core routing, and specialized air interfaces for high-throughput, low-latency, and high-density endpoints.

## Examples
A robotic manufacturing facility utilizes private 5G URLLC radio links with two-millisecond latency to coordinate autonomous mobile robots and overhead gantry cranes across twelve assembly lines.
