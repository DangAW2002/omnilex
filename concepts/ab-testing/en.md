## Easy explanation
Think of an A/B test like a bakery offering free samples of two slightly different cookie recipes to alternating customers entering the shop, counting which tray empties faster before deciding which recipe to bake in commercial quantities.

## Details
In digital product experimentation, incoming traffic is split randomly between Version A (the control baseline) and Version B (the experimental variant containing a targeted change, such as a redesigned call-to-action or altered pricing layout). Behavioral metrics—such as click-through rate, signup rate, or revenue per visitor—are tracked across both groups. By utilizing statistical hypothesis testing, product teams verify whether observed performance lifts represent genuine causal impact rather than random variance.

## Technical definition
A randomized controlled experiment in software optimization wherein two or more variants of a user interface or algorithmic flow are evaluated concurrently to establish statistical superiority.

## Examples
An online hotel platform tests a green booking button (Variant B) against its existing blue button (Control A), discovering that the green variant produces a statistically significant 4.2% increase in reservations.
