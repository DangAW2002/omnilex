## Easy explanation
Accrual accounting records sales the moment goods are handed over, and records bills the moment services are received, even if the actual bank transfer happens weeks later.

## Details
Contrasting with cash-basis bookkeeping, the accrual approach matches expenses directly against the revenues they helped generate during the same reporting period. This requires creating receivables for delivered orders awaiting payment and payables for incurred supplier costs, giving an accurate operational representation of ongoing enterprise profitability.

## Technical definition
The accounting convention mandating that transactions and economic events are recognized when performance obligations are met or obligations incurred rather than when cash is transferred.

## Examples
An IT consulting firm performs software audits in November and bills the client $30,000; it recognizes the $30,000 revenue in November even though client payment arrives in January.
