## Easy explanation
If an electronic neon sign flashes an advertisement five separate times while you stand waiting at a bus stop, you have personally generated five impressions for that ad.

## Details
An impression represents the atomic unit of digital ad delivery. It increments whenever an ad server transmits a creative asset to a publisher's webpage or mobile application and renders it within the browser DOM. Crucially, impressions count gross volume rather than net audience: if one individual refreshes a web page four times, the ad server logs four distinct impressions. Industry standards differentiate served impressions (the asset was downloaded) from viewable impressions (at least 50% of the creative was visible in the active viewport for at least one continuous second).

## Technical definition
A single instance of an advertising creative element being retrieved from a content delivery network and successfully painted within the client device screen viewport.

## Examples
A promoted post on a social media news feed passes through a user's screen in the morning and appears again in their feed during the evening commute, registering two impressions from that single user.
