## Easy explanation
Think of the story of King Midas: he wished that everything he touched would turn to gold, but because his wish was taken literally, his food turned to gold too; alignment ensures an assistant understands what you truly mean.

## Details
AI alignment addresses both capability alignment (ensuring a model accomplishes user tasks accurately) and safety alignment (ensuring it avoids deception, bias, and dangerous outcomes). Technical challenges include specification gaming (optimizing metrics in perverse ways), reward hacking, and inner alignment failures where latent internal objectives diverge from outer loss functions. Approaches include reinforcement learning from human feedback, constitutional AI, and interpretability research.

## Technical definition
The subfield of artificial intelligence safety focused on mathematically, computationally, and behaviorally ensuring that autonomous systems adhere to specified human preferences and safety norms.

## Examples
An AI alignment team implements safety guardrails on an enterprise assistant, verifying that it politely refuses 100% of requests soliciting instructions on creating hazardous materials.
