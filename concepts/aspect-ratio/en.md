## Easy explanation
Aspect ratio is like the shape of a picture frame: whether it is a square, a wide cinema rectangle, or a tall vertical postcard, the ratio tells you how broad the view is compared to its height.

## Details
Expressed as two numbers separated by a colon, aspect ratios dictate composition in photography, cinema, television, and web development. Traditional analog television adopted 4:3, modern digital high-definition standards standardized on 16:9, and widescreen cinema commonly shoots in 2.39:1 anamorphic format. The explosion of mobile social video introduced vertical 9:16 framing, forcing production houses to plan safe framing areas that accommodate multi-platform cropping.

## Technical definition
The mathematical ratio of the width to the height of a rectangular display area, sensor capture frame, or digital graphic asset.

## Examples
A video editor configures project sequence settings to 16:9 widescreen (1920x1080 pixels) for YouTube broadcast while creating a secondary 9:16 vertical crop (1080x1920 pixels) for mobile story distribution.
