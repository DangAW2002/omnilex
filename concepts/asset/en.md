## Easy explanation
An asset is anything valuable that a business owns or controls—like cash, equipment, delivery vans, or software patents—that helps it generate money.

## Details
Assets appear on the left or top of the balance sheet and are split between current items convertible to cash within twelve months, and non-current items like machinery and real estate. Under modern accounting frameworks, an item qualifies as an asset only if the business exercises control over it and can reliably quantify expected inflows of economic utility.

## Technical definition
A resource controlled by the enterprise as a consequence of past transactions from which probable future economic benefits are anticipated to flow.

## Examples
A distribution depot controls $500,000 in warehouse racking, $200,000 in electric delivery vans, and $150,000 in customer invoices due next month.
