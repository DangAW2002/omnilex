## Easy explanation
If an announcement is read aloud in an auditorium containing one hundred people, your reach is one hundred: repeating the announcement three times raises the impression count to three hundred, but your reach remains exactly one hundred people.

## Details
In digital advertising and social media analytics, reach measures the net audience size of unique individuals exposed to a campaign, eliminating duplications from repeat views. Measuring reach accurately presents technical challenges because modern consumers interact across multiple devices including mobile smartphones, tablets, work laptops, and connected televisions. Platforms deploy statistical deduplication models, unified login identifiers, and privacy-preserving identity graphs to map multiple device impressions to a single unique person. Advertisers balance reach against frequency: maximizing reach expands top-of-funnel brand awareness, whereas increasing frequency drives message retention.

## Technical definition
A media measurement metric representing the deduplicated aggregate count of unique authenticated profiles or statistically estimated distinct human observers within a target demographic exposed at least once to an ad delivery event.

## Examples
A smartphone company runs a product launch teaser on social video feeds that accumulates five million total impressions, but deduplicated audience analysis confirms that the video actually reached two million distinct individuals.
