## Easy explanation
Imagine academic research papers: whenever other scholars cite your published work as a helpful reference in their own bibliographies, your reputation among academic peers grows stronger.

## Details
Backlinks constitute one of the fundamental signals in search engine ranking algorithms, historically pioneered by Google's PageRank. When reputable and topically relevant domains link to a page, crawlers interpret the connection as a signal of trust and authority. Webmasters can qualify outgoing links using HTML `rel` attributes, distinguishing organic editorial endorsements from paid partnerships (`rel="sponsored"`) or user-contributed forum posts (`rel="ugc"`). Low-quality, reciprocal link farms or manipulative link schemes violate search policies and can lead to algorithmic devaluation.

## Technical definition
An inbound hyperlink embedded within a foreign origin document utilizing standard anchor markup pointing toward a destination uniform resource identifier on an independent host domain.

## Examples
A cybersecurity blog publishes a research breakdown of a zero-day exploit, and major technology news outlets link to the original article as their primary citation, boosting the author's domain visibility.
