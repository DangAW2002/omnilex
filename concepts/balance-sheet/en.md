## Easy explanation
Think of a balance sheet as a financial selfie taken on a single date, showing every item the business owns alongside every dollar borrowed or contributed by shareholders to acquire those things.

## Details
The report adheres strictly to the fundamental accounting equation where assets equal liabilities plus equity. It is divided into current items expected to turn into cash or be settled within one operating cycle or twelve months, and non-current items held for long-term production. Analysts review working balances to judge solvency and capital allocation.

## Technical definition
A point-in-time financial statement reporting cumulative economic resources controlled by an entity and the simultaneous legal and residual claims against those resources.

## Examples
A manufacturing firm reports $50 million in property and cash, balanced against $30 million in bank loans and vendor payables plus $20 million in contributed capital and retained earnings.
