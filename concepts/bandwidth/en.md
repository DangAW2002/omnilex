## Easy explanation
Like the width of a highway or water pipe: a six-lane highway lets more cars pass through every minute than a narrow one-lane road, but it does not make individual cars drive faster than their top speed.

## Details
In digital communications, bandwidth represents channel capacity measured in megabits per second (Mbps) or gigabits per second (Gbps). Higher bandwidth allows concurrent transmission of heavier data payloads (such as 4K video streams or large database backups). While bandwidth measures theoretical carrying capacity, realized throughput is bounded by latency, packet loss, and protocol overhead.

## Technical definition
The theoretical maximum throughput capacity of a logical or physical communication channel, quantified by the number of binary digits transmitted per unit time according to Shannon-Hartley channel capacity theorems.

## Examples
An office upgrades its optical internet connection from 100 Mbps to 1 Gbps, allowing 200 staff members to participate in high-definition video calls simultaneously without network buffering.
