## Easy explanation
Breaking news is like a fire alarm going off in the newsroom: regular programming stops immediately to report a major event unfolding right now.

## Details
When significant events occur, wire services and news organizations publish short initial dispatches labeled breaking news. As reporters gather confirmed facts from authorities, these preliminary alerts expand into detailed rolling coverage with live blogs and field broadcasts. Editorial teams must resist speculation and verify eyewitness accounts to prevent errors while competing against social media rumors.

## Technical definition
Unscheduled journalistic reporting of newly emerging, urgent events that interrupts scheduled broadcast programming or website content layouts.

## Examples
Television networks cut away from afternoon shows to broadcast live helicopter footage of a passenger aircraft executing a safe emergency landing.
