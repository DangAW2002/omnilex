## Easy explanation
Like packing a travel suitcase with a strict weight limit, a budget assigns every dollar of your paycheck a designated pocket before you travel through the month, preventing costly overages.

## Details
Creating a budget starts with totaling take-home income and subtracting essential fixed obligations like rent, loan payments, and utilities. Discretionary spending for entertainment and dining out receives an intentional ceiling. Surpluses are channeled directly into emergency reserves or retirement investments. Modern budgeters often utilize envelope budgeting, zero-based allocation, or automated mobile applications to reconcile actual receipts against planned targets.

## Technical definition
A quantitative schedule of expected financial inflows and projected resource expenditures over a discrete future operating interval, utilized to manage liquidity and maintain personal solvency.

## Examples
A household earning $4,000 net monthly allocates $2,000 to fixed housing and groceries, $800 to discretionary leisure, and transfers the remaining $1,200 to debt reduction and high-yield savings.
