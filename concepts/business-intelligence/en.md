## Easy explanation
Business intelligence is like having a digital control panel for a corporation. Rather than hunting through thousands of scattered spreadsheets, business executives look at organized charts showing sales figures, warehouse costs, and customer churn.

## Details
BI systems gather data from operational databases, sales CRMs, and supply chains into consolidated analytical stores. Analytical engines clean and aggregate these data feeds so non-technical analysts can filter metrics by month, branch, or product category. Modern BI includes interactive dashboards, ad-hoc query tools, automated anomaly alerts, and scheduled management summaries.

## Technical definition
A data engineering and analytics domain that transforms structured enterprise data into aggregated historical metrics, multidimensional OLAP cubes, and interactive management dashboards.

## Examples
A nationwide supermarket chain uses BI dashboards to compare daily dairy sales across 50 stores, discovering which promotions increased store visits without eroding profit margins.
