## Easy explanation
A byline is like an artist's signature in the corner of a painting: it clearly tells the audience whose reporting and writing they are reading.

## Details
The byline provides accountability and public transparency, attributing the reporting, writing, and analysis to designated journalists. It typically appears right below the headline or at the conclusion of the article, often accompanied by the writer's beat, email, or social handle. When several reporters collaborate across regions, publications use joint bylines or add contributing reporting credits at the article base.

## Technical definition
An attribution line displayed alongside a journalistic story naming the specific writer or field reporter who authored the work.

## Examples
Below the headline, the text "By Maria Nguyen in Geneva" informs readers that the Swiss diplomatic talks were covered by an eyewitness correspondent on site.
