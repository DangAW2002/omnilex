## Easy explanation
This statement tracks actual bank balance movements over time, showing where cash physically entered the business register and where cash was disbursed.

## Details
Because accounting income utilizes accruals, cash flow statements strip non-cash expenses like depreciation to reconcile balance sheet shifts. Cash movements are categorized into operations representing core business revenue, investing representing machinery or acquisitions, and financing representing loans, stock sales, and dividend distributions.

## Technical definition
A liquidity-centric financial report detailing net cash generated or consumed across standardized operating, investing, and capital-raising activities.

## Examples
A logistics business registers $5 million in net income but generates $8 million in operating cash because $3 million in depreciation required zero cash outflow this quarter.
