## Easy explanation
Think of churn rate as water leaking out of a bucket with holes: even if you keep pumping fresh water in from the top through marketing, you can never fill the bucket unless you patch the leaks causing existing customers to slip away.

## Details
Churn rate is a vital health metric for subscription and software-as-a-service (SaaS) businesses. It is calculated by dividing the number of customers lost during a period by the total customer count at the start of that period, expressed as a percentage. High churn indicates product friction, declining customer satisfaction, or intense competitive substitution. Product teams distinguish between voluntary churn (users deliberately canceling) and involuntary churn (payment card processing failures).

## Technical definition
The proportion of contractual subscribers or active users who discontinue their commercial engagement or subscription with a provider over a designated reporting interval.

## Examples
A cloud storage platform with 10,000 subscribers on the first day of the month loses 300 paying accounts by month-end, reflecting a monthly churn rate of (300 / 10,000) = 3%.
