## Easy explanation
Like putting an un-resettable digital thermometer inside an ice chest of medicine that beeps and sends a text message the moment the ice melts and the temperature creeps above four degrees Celsius.

## Details
Cold chain monitoring safeguards temperature-sensitive biologics, vaccines, and high-value perishable perishables between manufacture and point of consumption. Electronic data loggers and cellular/BLE sensor tags placed inside shipping insulated totes measure environmental parameters across temperature bands, such as ultra-low cryopreservation (minus eighty degrees Celsius), refrigerated cold storage (two to eight degrees Celsius), or frozen transport. If cooling compressor units malfunction or loading dock delays breach critical thresholds, the system calculates thermal exposure metrics and triggers immediate alarms before spoilage compromises efficacy.

## Technical definition
The continuous, auditable sensing, data logging, and wireless alerting framework designed to verify that temperature-sensitive commodities remain strictly within validated thermal limits throughout logistics handling and distribution.

## Examples
A pharmaceutical logistics distributor equips vaccine shipping coolers with BLE temperature loggers that broadcast thermal metrics to the refrigerated truck's LTE-M gateway, triggering immediate SMS alerts if interior temperatures rise above six degrees Celsius.
