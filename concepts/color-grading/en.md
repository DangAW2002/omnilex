## Easy explanation
Color grading is like putting mood lighting on a movie scene: warm golden hues make a summer memory feel nostalgic, while cold cyan tones turn a city street into a tense thriller.

## Details
Post-production workflows separate technical color correction (neutralizing color casts and matching exposure across clips) from creative color grading. Colorists use dedicated grading suites to sculpt lift, gamma, gain, and hue curves. Working in color-managed environments like ACES with wide dynamic range RAW footage, artists apply custom palettes, film grain emulations, and selective masks to direct viewer attention toward focal points.

## Technical definition
The creative post-production phase of altering luminance, chrominance, and color balance to achieve deliberate aesthetic atmospheres, narrative stylization, or brand palettes.

## Examples
A feature film colorist pushes shadows into deep teal and highlights into warm orange tones to give an action sequence a stylized, cinematic blockbuster visual appearance.
