## Easy explanation
Like a rolling snowball: as it tumbles down the hill, it picks up snow faster and faster because it keeps getting bigger.

## Details
Every compounding period, accrued interest is added to the principal balance. Subsequent calculations determine interest from the enlarged sum, resulting in exponential growth over time.

## Technical definition
The addition of interest to the principal sum of a loan or deposit, so that the added interest also earns interest from that point forward.

## Examples
Investing $10,000 at 7% annual compounding produces:
- Year 1: $10,700
- Year 2: $11,449 (an extra $49 from interest on interest)
- Year 10: $19,671.51
