## Easy explanation
Think of granting eyes and visual comprehension to a computer, so that when it looks at a photo of a busy street, it knows which objects are pedestrians, road signs, or bicycles.

## Details
Computer vision transforms unstructured arrays of numerical pixel values into high-level semantic representations. Classical pipelines relied on manual feature extraction like edge filters and corner detectors, whereas modern architectures leverage deep convolutional neural networks and vision transformers. Prominent subtasks include image classification, object detection with bounding boxes, semantic segmentation, visual tracking, and 3D scene reconstruction.

## Technical definition
The automated algorithmic derivation of explicit physical descriptions, classifications, and structural relationships from digital images and multi-frame video sequences.

## Examples
An automated highway toll camera captures vehicle images at 80 km/h, isolating the rectangular license plate area and reading the alphanumeric characters in 40 milliseconds.
