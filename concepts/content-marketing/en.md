## Easy explanation
Instead of constantly interrupting people with sales flyers, a gardening tools company publishes a free monthly illustrated guide on how to grow sweet tomatoes at home, so gardeners naturally think of that brand whenever they need new pruning shears.

## Details
Content marketing shifts organizational focus from overt transactional pitching to delivering genuine informational and educational value. Assets encompass blog essays, video walkthroughs, podcast episodes, downloadable industry research, and interactive templates. Rather than relying entirely on recurring paid advertising budgets, authoritative content assets compound in organic reach over time, earning backlinks, brand recall, and inbound search ranking. Search engines like Google prioritize helpful, reliable, people-first content written for human readers rather than manipulative search engine optimization formulas. By consistently answering consumer queries and solving domain challenges, brands nurture long-term customer loyalty and establish category thought leadership.

## Technical definition
A strategic marketing management methodology centered on the planned ideation, production, curation, and syndication of editorial media assets engineered to satisfy user intent and cultivate commercially profitable customer engagement.

## Examples
A cloud database provider creates an in-depth video tutorial series comparing distributed SQL database architectures, attracting three hundred thousand software engineers and establishing the company as the premier authority in database engineering.
