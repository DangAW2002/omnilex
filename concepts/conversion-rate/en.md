## Easy explanation
If one hundred customers walk into a shoe store and three of them walk up to the cash register and purchase shoes, the store's conversion rate for that afternoon is exactly three percent.

## Details
Conversion rate measures how effectively commercial websites and landing pages turn inbound web traffic into tangible business value. A conversion can represent a macro-conversion (such as completing an e-commerce checkout, subscribing to a software plan, or requesting a commercial sales quote) or a micro-conversion (such as downloading an informational whitepaper or creating an account). Calculated mathematically by dividing total verified conversions by total site visits or clicks and multiplying by one hundred, conversion rate highlights user experience bottlenecks, checkout friction, and pricing mismatches. Conversion rate optimization (CRO) systematically tests copy, layout, and value propositions via A/B testing to lift this metric.

## Technical definition
A fundamental digital analytics effectiveness ratio computed as the total count of successfully executed goal events divided by the aggregate number of qualifying user sessions or ad clicks.

## Examples
An online coffee supplier logs 10,000 unique user sessions on its store over one week and records 250 successful checkout transactions, delivering an e-commerce conversion rate of 2.5%.
