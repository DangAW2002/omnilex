## Easy explanation
Copyright is like a legal lock on your creative work: because you wrote the book or painted the canvas, nobody else can sell copies without your permission.

## Details
Copyright vests automatically the instant an original creative work becomes fixed in a tangible form, such as on paper, hard drive, or audio tape. Under international treaties like the Berne Convention, protection extends across borders without mandatory state registration. Copyright divides into economic rights (reproducing, performing, translating, and distributing for profit) and moral rights (the enduring right to be credited and prevent derogatory mutilation).

## Technical definition
A legal framework granting creators exclusive statutory monopolies over the reproduction, distribution, public performance, and adaptation of original literary, musical, and artistic works.

## Examples
A novelist retains copyright over their manuscript, granting a publisher exclusive rights to print paperbacks while licensing separate rights to a film studio for a cinematic adaptation.
