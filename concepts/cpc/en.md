## Easy explanation
Think of a store that only pays the delivery courier when a customer actually opens the door and takes the package inside, rather than paying just to have the delivery van drive past the house.

## Details
Cost per click is the standard commercial billing framework for search engine advertising platforms like Google Ads and Microsoft Advertising. Advertisers configure a maximum CPC bid representing the highest price they are willing to pay for an individual visitor. During real-time auctions, the actual CPC billed is typically determined through second-price auction dynamics combined with quality score weighting, meaning an advertiser frequently pays just enough to beat the next highest competing ad rank.

## Technical definition
A pay-per-click billing settlement mechanism and unit economic metric determined by total advertising expenditure divided by the aggregate count of verified inbound click events.

## Examples
A dental clinic spends $500 on Google Search ads targeting emergency appointments and generates 100 clicks from prospective patients, resulting in an average cost per click of $5.
