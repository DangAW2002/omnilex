## Easy explanation
The CPI tracks the total price tag of an imaginary shopping cart containing everyday items like bread, electricity, rent, and bus fares to see how living costs change.

## Details
Statistical statistical bureaus collect thousands of retail quotes monthly, assigning relative weightings based on typical household consumption surveys. Comparing current basket totals against historic reference periods calculates the annualized headline rate of consumer inflation used for indexing pensions and collective labor contracts.

## Technical definition
A Laspeyres-type or chained weighted price index monitoring the temporal cost variations of a fixed representative basket of household consumption commodities.

## Examples
When the national CPI index climbs from 120.0 to 123.6 over twelve months, it documents an annual consumer inflation rate of 3.0 percent.
