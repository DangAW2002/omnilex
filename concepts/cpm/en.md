## Easy explanation
Think of renting space on a highway billboard: you pay a flat rental rate based on the estimated thousand cars that drive past each day, regardless of whether any driver stops to call the phone number on the sign.

## Details
Derived from the Latin word mille meaning thousand, CPM represents the traditional buying currency for display banners, programmatic video networks, and social media branding campaigns. Advertisers choose CPM when their primary objective is broad visibility and repeated brand exposure rather than direct transactional response. Ad servers track every valid ad render; once one thousand verified views accumulate, one unit of CPM cost is debited. Modern ad networks increasingly utilize viewable CPM (vCPM) to guarantee that at least fifty percent of the creative pixels remained in viewport for a minimum duration.

## Technical definition
A media buying valuation standard specifying the financial expenditure incurred for every 1,000 recorded ad rendering units across display, video, or social inventory.

## Examples
A soft drink brand launches a nationwide summer campaign buying 2,000,000 video banner impressions at a CPM of $6, incurring a total marketing placement budget of $12,000.
