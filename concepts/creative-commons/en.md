## Easy explanation
Creative Commons is like a buffet menu for copyright: instead of saying "all rights reserved", creators mark their work with simple symbols telling everyone exactly how they can share or use it.

## Details
Creative Commons licenses sit on top of underlying copyright, allowing creators to retain ownership while granting advance public permissions. Licensors select from four core conditions: Attribution (BY), ShareAlike (SA), NonCommercial (NC), and NoDerivatives (ND). Combining these modular clauses yields six primary licenses, alongside CC0 for dedicating works to the public domain. Platforms like Wikipedia and open-access scientific journals rely heavily on these licenses to foster global collaboration.

## Technical definition
A suite of free, legally enforceable copyright licenses offering standardized permissions to copy, distribute, and remix intellectual works under human-readable and machine-readable metadata conditions.

## Examples
A photographer uploads landscape pictures under a CC BY-SA license, allowing bloggers to publish the images freely provided they credit the photographer and release derivative works under identical terms.
