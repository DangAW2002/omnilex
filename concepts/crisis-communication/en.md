## Easy explanation
Crisis communication is like emergency dispatch during a storm: leaders must share clear, calm, and honest instructions so people stay safe and do not panic from rumors.

## Details
When organizations confront environmental disasters, product recalls, data breaches, or legal scandals, crisis communication protocols activate immediately. Specialists establish a centralized war room to gather facts, monitor social media sentiment, and coordinate public briefings. Best practices require swift acknowledgment of responsibility, empathetic concern for affected parties, and transparent descriptions of remediation steps rather than defensive denials.

## Technical definition
A specialized subfield of public relations dedicated to designing and executing strategic communications that mitigate organizational harm during acute operational crises.

## Examples
Following an airline computer outage that grounded thousands of flights, the corporate communications division issues hourly video updates explaining recovery progress and detailing automatic passenger refunds.
