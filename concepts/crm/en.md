## Easy explanation
Think of a CRM system as a shared digital address book with memory. Instead of contact details scattered across personal phones and sticky notes, every phone call, email exchange, and support question is recorded in one place for the whole team to see.

## Details
A CRM platform tracks leads as they advance through a sales pipeline, records meeting notes, and automates routine follow-up tasks. Marketing, sales, and customer service teams share the same chronological history, preventing duplicate outreach and missed commitments. Modern platforms also offer pipeline forecasting, contract storage, and customer health scoring.

## Technical definition
An enterprise software suite designed to manage organization-wide interactions with prospects and customers through consolidated contact repositories, pipeline tracking, and customer lifecycle automation.

## Examples
A software company uses its CRM to route incoming trial signups to account executives and notify them when an enterprise lead requests a security assessment.
