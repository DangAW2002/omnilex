## Easy explanation
If one hundred people walk past a billboard announcing a free concert and five of them actually tear off a flyer slip to take home, your response rate is five percent: that is exactly what CTR measures online.

## Details
CTR serves as an essential efficiency gauge across paid advertising, organic search, and email marketing. Calculated by dividing total recorded clicks by total ad impressions and multiplying by one hundred, CTR reflects how enticing, relevant, and compelling an offer appears to its intended audience. In ad networks like Google Ads, high click-through rates positively influence ad rank algorithms and lower effective cost-per-click, signaling to the platform that users find the advertisement valuable.

## Technical definition
A performance metric expressing the frequency with which an impression converts into an intentional user navigation event, calculated mathematically as total clicks divided by total served impressions.

## Examples
An e-commerce shoe retailer displays a banner ad that appears 10,000 times on sports blogs and receives 350 user clicks, yielding a click-through rate of 3.5%.
