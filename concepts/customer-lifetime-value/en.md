## Easy explanation
Think of customer lifetime value as the difference between a one-time tourist buying a single coffee and a neighborhood resident who stops by every morning for five years: the regular neighbor represents vastly higher total value to the café owner over time.

## Details
Customer lifetime value estimates the financial worth of a customer relationship. In subscription models, a foundational formula multiplies Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) by the gross margin, divided by the customer churn rate. Alternatively, it equals average purchase value multiplied by purchase frequency and average customer lifespan. Comparing CLV directly against Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) governs marketing efficiency: healthy software businesses maintain an LTV-to-CAC ratio of at least 3:1.

## Technical definition
A financial projection forecasting the net aggregate margin or revenue contributed by an individual customer account across the entirety of their commercial lifecycle with a firm.

## Examples
A streaming platform with an average monthly fee of $15, a 70% gross margin, and a monthly churn rate of 2% calculates customer lifetime value as ($15 × 0.70) / 0.02 = $525 per subscriber.
