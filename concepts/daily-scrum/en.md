## Easy explanation
Imagine a surgical team pausing for a brief sixty-second huddle before an operation to confirm patient details, verify instruments, and align roles so everyone moves with synchronized precision.

## Details
The Daily Scrum takes place at the same time and location every workday. It is designed specifically by and for the developers executing the sprint backlog. Traditionally framed around three questions—what was done yesterday, what will be addressed today, and what impediments exist—the meeting focuses squarely on progressing toward the sprint goal. Detailed architectural debates or side discussions are deferred to separate breakout sessions to keep the gathering concise.

## Technical definition
A timeboxed 15-minute event held each working day of a sprint in which the developers check how close they are to the sprint goal and adjust their plan for the remaining sprint backlog.

## Examples
At 9:30 AM, an engineering team gathers around their virtual board; one developer reports finishing API authentication, while another flags a blocking test environment issue requiring help.
