## Easy explanation
Like city traffic laws, street signs, and driving licenses: they ensure vehicles move safely, prevent reckless driving, and ensure only certified drivers operate heavy machinery.

## Details
Data governance establishes formal policies, ownership accountabilities, stewardship processes, and security controls across an organization's data assets. It defines who can access sensitive customer records, ensures schema standards are respected across teams, catalogs business terminology, and tracks compliance with statutory legal regulations like privacy mandates and financial disclosure audits.

## Technical definition
The strategic framework, policies, processes, and metric standards that define authority, accountability, and quality control over organizational data assets throughout their lifecycle.

## Examples
A commercial bank enforces data governance rules requiring customer national ID numbers to be masked in development databases and audited quarterly.
