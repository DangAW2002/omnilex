## Easy explanation
Data visualization is translating boring columns of numbers into pictures. Looking at a spreadsheet of 10,000 temperature readings makes your eyes blur, but plotting them on a line graph instantly shows when a heatwave happened.

## Details
Visualizing data maps numerical variables onto graphical properties such as position, length, shape, and color palettes. Common chart formats include bar charts for discrete comparisons, line graphs for time series, scatter plots for correlations, and heatmaps for matrix densities. Effective designs follow visual hierarchy rules, avoid misleading truncated axes, and ensure accessibility for color-blind viewers.

## Technical definition
The graphical representation of abstract data structures and mathematical variables via encoded visual channels such as spatial coordinates, marks, color ramps, and geographic geometry.

## Examples
An analyst maps ride-hailing pickups across city districts into a color-graded density heatmap, highlighting peak commuter congestion around subway stations at 6 PM.
