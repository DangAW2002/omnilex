## Easy explanation
A dataset is like a textbook full of solved exercises and practice questions that a student studies before taking an independent final examination.

## Details
Datasets consist of individual samples comprising features (independent variables) and optional target labels. Practitioners partition datasets into distinct training sets for parameter fitting, validation sets for hyperparameter selection and checkpoint tuning, and held-out test sets for unbiased evaluation. Rigorous data curation involves handling missing attributes, normalizing scales, deduplication, and removing toxic or skewed records.

## Technical definition
A bounded, indexed corpus of empirical records characterized by measurable dimensional attributes, arranged for statistical modeling and algorithmic evaluation.

## Examples
A medical archive containing fifty thousand labeled chest radiographs paired with clinical diagnostic reports used to train automated screening classifiers.
