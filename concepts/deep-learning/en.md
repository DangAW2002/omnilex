## Easy explanation
While shallow algorithms need humans to manually measure and outline key characteristics beforehand, deep learning works like human perception: directly analyzing raw sounds or images layer by layer to understand them.

## Details
Deep learning models deploy cascades of successive hidden layers. Lower layers capture localized patterns such as edges, acoustic frequencies, or word stems; intermediate layers compose textures, phonemes, or phrases; higher layers synthesize abstract semantics. This eliminates tedious manual feature extraction. The methodology underpins breakthroughs in speech transcription, computer vision, translation, and large generative foundations.

## Technical definition
A subfield of machine learning founded on multi-tiered artificial neural networks that learn distributed, non-linear representations across successive hierarchical transformations.

## Examples
An autonomous driving perception stack feeding multi-camera video streams directly into a deep neural model to plan navigation trajectories in real time without handcrafted vision rules.
