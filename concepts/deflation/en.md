## Easy explanation
Deflation is when prices across the economy keep dropping, making things cheaper but often causing businesses to freeze hiring and cut wages.

## Details
While falling prices sound appealing, broad deflation triggers severe macroeconomic hazards. Shoppers postpone purchases anticipating lower prices tomorrow, dampening commercial sales and corporate profits. Because existing debt contracts remain fixed in nominal amounts, paying back loans becomes progressively more crushing in real terms.

## Technical definition
A macroeconomic phenomenon where the rate of change in aggregate price level falls below zero percent over an extended timeframe.

## Examples
During Japan’s economic stagnation in the late 1990s, consumer prices contracted by 1 percent annually, discouraging corporate capital investments.
