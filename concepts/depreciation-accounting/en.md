## Easy explanation
Instead of recording the full expense of a expensive delivery truck in the month it was bought, depreciation divides that cost across all the years the truck will work.

## Details
Adhering to the matching principle, depreciation spreads upfront capital expenditures to match the multi-year revenues generated by those physical assets. Straight-line depreciation deducts equal amounts annually, whereas accelerated methods front-load deductions. Accumulated depreciation reduces the carrying net book value of assets on the balance sheet.

## Technical definition
An accounting convention allocating the capitalized historic acquisition cost of a tangible non-current asset minus residual value across expected operating service periods.

## Examples
A printing press purchased for $100,000 with a 10-year lifespan and zero salvage value generates a annual straight-line depreciation expense of $10,000.
