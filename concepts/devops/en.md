## Easy explanation
Like a kitchen where the chefs who cook the food work hand-in-hand with the waiters who serve it, sharing feedback constantly instead of blaming each other when a dish is delayed.

## Details
DevOps emerged as a response to the traditional friction between software developers (focused on shipping frequent changes) and operations engineers (focused on preserving system stability). By adopting shared goals, cross-functional responsibilities, and automated toolchains, organizations remove barriers between writing code and managing production infrastructure. Core practices include continuous integration, continuous delivery, infrastructure as code, automated testing, and comprehensive monitoring. When implemented successfully, DevOps drastically reduces release lead times, accelerates defect detection, and fosters a high-trust collaborative engineering culture.

## Technical definition
A collaborative cultural philosophy and operational framework integrating software engineering and IT operations through shared automation, telemetry, and continuous feedback.

## Examples
A technology company shifts from quarterly manual releases to deploying production updates fifteen times a day through fully automated CI/CD pipelines managed jointly by developers and operators.
