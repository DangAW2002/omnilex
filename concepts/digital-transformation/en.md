## Easy explanation
Digital transformation is not just buying computers for the office; it is reinventing how an organization works from the ground up. Think of a taxi fleet replacing paper logbooks, telephone dispatches, and cash payments with a smartphone app that manages bookings and digital payments instantly.

## Details
Digital transformation goes beyond digitizing paper records. It fundamentally re-evaluates customer experiences, internal operational speed, and business models using cloud computing, automation, and real-time analytics. Successful programs require organizational restructuring, agile operational experimentation, and upskilling staff to work collaboratively with modern software systems.

## Technical definition
The comprehensive modernization of an enterprise's organizational workflows, architectural infrastructure, and value delivery models through strategic adoption of cloud native platforms, automated workflows, and data capabilities.

## Examples
A traditional government licensing agency replaces paper mail applications with an online identity portal, shrinking citizen permit waiting periods from six weeks to fifteen minutes.
