## Easy explanation
Double-entry bookkeeping means every time money moves, it is written down in two places: where it came from and where it went, making sure both sides balance.

## Details
Pioneered formally by Luca Pacioli, this foundation of financial reporting mandates that every debit entry must be accompanied by an equal and opposing credit entry. This duality ensures that the fundamental balance sheet equation always remains balanced, preventing accidental omission and creating an audit trail.

## Technical definition
An accounting system based on the duality principle where total debits entered across all general ledger accounts must mathematically equal total credits.

## Examples
When a bakery buys $500 of flour on vendor credit, it debits raw materials inventory by $500 and credits accounts payable by $500.
