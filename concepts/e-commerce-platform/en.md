## Easy explanation
An e-commerce platform is an all-in-one digital store kit. It provides the digital shelves for showcasing goods, the virtual shopping basket where customers gather items, and the checkout counter for collecting shipping details and payments.

## Details
E-commerce platforms manage the full digital shopping journey. Key features include product categorization, promotional coupon engines, persistent shopping carts, tax calculations, and order status tracking. Modern platforms also integrate with shipping carriers for automated parcel tracking and provide analytics on checkout drop-off rates. They range from hosted SaaS services to modular composable systems powered by APIs.

## Technical definition
An integrated commerce application suite providing storefront presentation layers, shopping cart persistence, transactional checkout engines, and product information management capabilities.

## Examples
An athletic shoe brand launches a regional web shop on a hosted e-commerce platform, managing 500 shoe styles, localized currency displays, and courier dispatch.
