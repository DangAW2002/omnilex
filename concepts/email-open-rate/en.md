## Easy explanation
If an envelope containing a personal invitation is delivered to one hundred mailboxes on your street, and twenty-five neighbors actually slice open the envelope to see what is inside, your open rate is twenty-five percent.

## Details
In email marketing operations, open rate functions as a primary barometer of sender reputation, sender name recognition, and subject line resonance. Historically measured by embedding an invisible, transparent one-pixel image (tracking pixel) inside the email HTML body, an open is logged when the client application downloads that graphic file from the host server. Modern inbox privacy controls, such as Apple's MPP safeguards, automatically preload and store cached image assets on proxy relays, inflating nominal open rates. Consequently, digital marketers treat open rates as relative directional indicators rather than absolute exact counts, pairing them with downstream click-through rates and conversion telemetry to evaluate campaign health.

## Technical definition
The mathematical ratio of unique recipient client image rendering requests triggered by an HTML tracking beacon divided by the net count of successfully delivered email transmissions.

## Examples
An online education provider transmits an email course announcement to 50,000 active subscribers; after 48 hours, analytics platforms verify that 12,500 subscribers opened the message, producing an email open rate of 25.0%.
