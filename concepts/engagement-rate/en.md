## Easy explanation
If an art gallery host presents ten paintings to fifty visitors and twenty people stop to ask questions, take photos, or leave comments in the guestbook, your gallery's engagement rate is forty percent.

## Details
Engagement rate evaluates whether content genuinely resonates with viewers beyond passive scrolling. On social networks like Instagram, TikTok, and LinkedIn, engagement aggregates actions such as likes, comments, shares, saves, and link taps, typically divided by total reach or followers and expressed as a percentage. In web analytics environments such as Google Analytics 4 (GA4), an engaged session is formally defined as a user visit that lasts longer than 10 seconds, triggers a key conversion event, or views at least two distinct pages. Algorithms on modern content recommendation feeds rely heavily on high engagement velocity to determine whether a post should be amplified to broader audiences.

## Technical definition
A standardized quantitative ratio computing the aggregate sum of discrete user interaction telemetry events divided by the qualifying exposure baseline across a defined content asset.

## Examples
A tech brand posts an unboxing video that reaches 20,000 unique viewers on social media and generates 600 likes, 150 comments, and 250 shares, delivering an overall engagement rate of 5.0%.
