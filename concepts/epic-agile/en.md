## Easy explanation
Think of an epic as a full book containing multiple distinct chapters: you read each chapter one at a time over several evenings, but the entire narrative only comes together once all chapters are finished.

## Details
Epics provide a conceptual bridge connecting high-level product roadmaps with day-to-day engineering tasks. Because an epic represents a substantial capability—such as launching an international checkout system—it is far too bulky to fit inside a single sprint. Product managers and engineers decompose an epic into manageable, independently deployable user stories that squads implement across consecutive iterations. Progress is tracked via cumulative burndown or progress charts.

## Technical definition
A macro-level requirement container in agile methodologies that groups related user stories sharing a unified strategic objective across multiple development iterations.

## Examples
An airline app creates an epic called "Revamp Seat Selection Experience," containing stories for 3D cabin rendering, extra legroom pricing tiers, and accessible seating reservations.
