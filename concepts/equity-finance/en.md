## Easy explanation
Equity is what actually belongs to the business founders and shareholders once every creditor, vendor, and lender has been paid in full.

## Details
Representing the residual claim on corporate assets, equity comprises original stock capital contributed by investors plus cumulative retained earnings saved across profitable periods, less share buybacks and dividends. It serves as a financial shock absorber protecting creditors from operating losses.

## Technical definition
The residual claim on the assets of an enterprise that remains after deducting all obligations to external creditors.

## Examples
If a startup holds $12 million in hardware and customer receivables and owes $4 million to banks, the net equity standing to shareholders is $8 million.
