## Easy explanation
An ERP system functions as the central nervous system of an enterprise. When a customer places an order, the system automatically checks factory inventory, schedules supplier shipments, and updates the accounting ledger without manual data re-entry.

## Details
ERP software consolidates business processes that once operated in isolated department silos. Core modules cover general ledger, accounts payable and receivable, supply chain procurement, warehouse tracking, and manufacturing schedules. Because every transaction draws from a single database, leadership views real-time operational status without waiting for monthly manual reconciliation.

## Technical definition
A centralized enterprise software architecture that unifies transactional operational processes across accounting, supply chain, human capital, and manufacturing into a shared relational schema and transactional ledger.

## Examples
A furniture manufacturer receives an order for 200 office chairs; the ERP automatically reserves upholstery fabrics from warehouse bins and generates purchase orders for missing steel frames.
