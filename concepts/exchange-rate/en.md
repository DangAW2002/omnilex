## Easy explanation
The exchange rate is the price of money: it tells you exactly how many Japanese yen, US dollars, or euros you get when you trade in your local cash.

## Details
Determined in global foreign exchange markets through the interplay of international trade flows, interest rate differentials, and sovereign capital investments, exchange rates fluctuate continuously. Fixed peg regimes require central bank currency interventions, whereas floating regimes let market supply and demand set equilibrium prices.

## Technical definition
The bilateral relative price at which two sovereign currencies are traded in international spot or forward financial foreign exchange venues.

## Examples
If the USD/VND rate stands at 25,400, an importer must pay 25,400 Vietnamese dong to acquire one US dollar for cross-border equipment procurement.
