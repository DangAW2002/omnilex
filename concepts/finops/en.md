## Easy explanation
Like families reviewing a detailed itemized monthly electricity and grocery statement: rather than just complaining when the bill is high, parents and children work together to turn off unused lights and choose smarter bulk plans.

## Details
FinOps combines finance, engineering, and business operations to drive visibility and cost optimization across cloud services. Because cloud resources can be spun up by developers in seconds, FinOps introduces automated cost dashboards, unit economics metrics (like cost per customer transaction), tagging policies, and rightsizing recommendations to ensure cloud spending drives measurable business value.

## Technical definition
A collaborative cloud financial management discipline defined by the FinOps Foundation that unifies technology, finance, and product teams to maximize business value through data-driven spending decisions.

## Examples
A fintech engineering department implements a FinOps practice with automated tagging on all cloud resources, reducing annual AWS expenditures by 25% by downsizing over-provisioned idle development instances.
