## Easy explanation
GDP is the master scorecard for an entire country’s economic output, adding up the price tag of every car made, haircut given, and software license sold within its borders over a year.

## Details
Economists track GDP through three equal perspectives: expenditure (consumer spending, gross business investment, government purchases, and net exports), production output, and earned income. Real GDP eliminates inflationary price noise, enabling economists to ascertain genuine physical economic expansion or contraction across quarters.

## Technical definition
A comprehensive national macroeconomic indicator measuring the aggregate monetary market value of all final production output generated within geographic borders.

## Examples
The economic statistics office reports national real GDP expanded by 6.5 percent year-over-year driven by booming semiconductor manufacturing and retail sales.
