## Easy explanation
Think of labeled filing folders in an office cabinet: stamping "#Travel" across the top of various photos and diary entries groups them into one convenient drawer where anyone interested in traveling finds them immediately.

## Details
Originally popularized on Twitter and subsequently adopted across YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, and LinkedIn, a hashtag converts unformatted strings preceded by the `#` symbol into interactive hypertext links without spaces. When a user clicks or searches a hashtag, the platform aggregates all public messages carrying that specific identifier into a dynamic feed sorted chronologically or by engagement relevance. Marketers deploy branded hashtags to monitor user-generated campaigns, community conversations, and live event participation. While valuable for contextual discovery, over-tagging can dilute relevance and trigger spam filters on discovery feeds.

## Technical definition
A user-generated semantic indexing token consisting of an unbroken alphanumeric string preceded by the octothorpe character, dynamically parsed by platform engines into a topical hyperlinked taxonomy locator.

## Examples
A photography enthusiast shares a landscape shot of the Grand Canyon and includes "#LandscapePhotography" in the post description so other photographers exploring that specific topic feed can discover and comment on the image.
