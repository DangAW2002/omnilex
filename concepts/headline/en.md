## Easy explanation
Think of a headline as the shop sign above a storefront: it tells passersby what is waiting inside before they ever cross the threshold.

## Details
Headlines serve a dual role in print and digital newsrooms: conveying the news accurately while enticing readers to engage. Copy editors write headlines after reporters finish drafting, fitting them within strict character budgets. In digital journalism, headline formulation incorporates search optimization and social sharing performance, yet reputable outlets avoid misleading phrasing to maintain reader trust.

## Technical definition
A prominent title or heading displayed above a journalistic piece, formatted in bold typography to identify the article's subject and signal its news value.

## Examples
A morning newspaper banner headline might proclaim "Central Bank Lowers Benchmark Interest Rate by Half a Point", instantly communicating the day's primary economic development.
