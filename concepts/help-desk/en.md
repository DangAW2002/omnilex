## Easy explanation
A help desk is like a hospital reception triage desk for computer issues. When something breaks, users submit a request, receive a ticket number, and wait for a support technician to diagnose and fix the problem.

## Details
Help desk systems organize incoming technical inquiries from multiple channels such as email, phone, and web portals. Each inquiry becomes a numbered ticket with assigned urgency, category, and ownership. Technicians track updates, share diagnostic notes, and escalate thorny issues to senior engineering groups. Most solutions also include a searchable self-service knowledge base to help users solve routine issues independently.

## Technical definition
A centralized incident intake and service request management system that classifies, assigns, tracks, and measures the resolution lifecycle of user operational problems against agreed service standards.

## Examples
An employee whose laptop cannot connect to the corporate Wi-Fi files a help desk ticket, which is automatically assigned to the on-site office IT technician.
