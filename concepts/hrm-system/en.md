## Easy explanation
An HRMS functions as an electronic personnel office. Staff can submit vacation requests and view monthly pay stubs from a self-service app, while managers track department headcounts and reviews.

## Details
HRMS suites centralize the employee lifecycle from onboarding through departure. They automate time-off requests, compute payroll deductions, administer insurance enrollment, and securely store compliance documents. Modern platforms include self-service portals where employees update personal details and submit expenses directly, reducing administrative paperwork for human resources staff.

## Technical definition
An enterprise information platform that consolidates personnel records, payroll computation engines, workforce scheduling, and statutory compliance reporting across the employee lifecycle.

## Examples
A logistics company uses its HRMS to verify driver leave balances, approve substitute shifts, and calculate monthly overtime allowances for 400 warehouse workers.
