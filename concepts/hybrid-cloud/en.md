## Easy explanation
Like keeping your gold jewelry inside a secure home safe while depositing liquid cash into a commercial bank account for daily transactions.

## Details
A hybrid cloud architecture establishes encrypted VPNs or dedicated fiber cross-connects between enterprise data centers and public cloud availability zones. This architecture allows organizations to keep highly sensitive or regulated datasets on self-managed hardware while bursting ephemeral computing jobs or public web endpoints into elastic public cloud capacity during traffic surges.

## Technical definition
A composite cloud environment composed of distinct private and public cloud infrastructures that remain unique entities yet are bound together by standardized or proprietary technology that enables data and application portability.

## Examples
A retail bank keeps its primary ledger databases in its private on-premises data center to comply with banking regulations, while deploying its customer-facing mobile banking application on public cloud instances to absorb holiday traffic peaks.
