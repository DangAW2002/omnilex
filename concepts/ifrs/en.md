## Easy explanation
IFRS is like a shared international language for corporate financial reports, allowing investors in London, Tokyo, or New York to compare companies fairly.

## Details
Administered by the London-based IASB council, IFRS standards emphasize underlying economic substance and fair value measurement over strict legal forms. Mandated or permitted in over 140 countries, adopting IFRS lowers capital access costs for cross-border corporations by eliminating the need to prepare multiple restated local financial statements.

## Technical definition
A standardized set of principles-based international accounting standards governing the recognition, measurement, presentation, and disclosure of transactions in general-purpose financial reports.

## Examples
A multinational airline standardizes aircraft operating leases on its consolidated balance sheet according to IFRS 16 across European and Asian subsidiaries.
