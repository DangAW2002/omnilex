## Easy explanation
Like equipping an entire automobile manufacturing plant with a nervous system, where every stamping press, paint robot, and conveyor belt shares vital signs to prevent breakdowns and optimize energy consumption.

## Details
IIoT bridges the historical divide between Operational Technology (OT) on factory floors and Information Technology (IT) in corporate cloud environments. By outfitting industrial assets—such as turbines, pumps, and CNC machines—with smart sensors and edge gateways running protocols like OPC UA and MQTT, facilities capture real-time telemetry including temperature, pressure, acoustic emissions, and vibration. These data pipelines feed advanced analytical platforms that drive automated process tuning, predictive maintenance, and supply chain synchronization while enforcing strict industrial cybersecurity frameworks like IEC 62443.

## Technical definition
The integration of interconnected sensors, advanced instruments, and computing devices within industrial systems, enabling real-time telemetry acquisition, cloud-level analytics, and autonomous operational control.

## Examples
A steel fabrication plant connects sixty industrial induction furnaces via IIoT gateways, streaming power draw and temperature curves to machine learning models that predict heating coil degradation before catastrophic melting occurs.
