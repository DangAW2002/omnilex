## Easy explanation
Like a sudden water pipe bursting in an office building: building maintenance stops everyday tasks immediately to shut off the main valve and repair the damage.

## Details
When systems crash or exceed critical latency thresholds, teams declare an incident. Structured incident management frameworks assign roles such as Incident Commander (coordinating response), Communications Lead (updating customers), and Operations Lead (diagnosing and restoring service), prioritizing quick mitigation over root-cause investigation.

## Technical definition
An unscheduled operational state characterized by a breach of service availability, data consistency, or security baselines requiring emergency remediation.

## Examples
A primary database failure takes down checkout functionality on an e-commerce platform for 32 minutes, triggering a severity-1 incident response.
