## Easy explanation
The income statement works like a film recording operations over several months, tallying incoming sales receipts and subtracting production costs and taxes to see if money was made.

## Details
Structured top-down starting with gross sales revenue, it deducts cost of goods sold to reveal gross profit, operating overhead for operating margin, and non-operating financing charges and corporate taxes to reach net income. It measures operational economic performance under accrual guidelines rather than simple bank account deposits.

## Technical definition
A periodic financial statement measuring the economic transformation of inputs to revenue to determine net performance over a discrete reporting timeframe.

## Examples
A software company posts $10 million in subscription sales, subtracts $3 million in cloud costs and $5 million in payroll, resulting in a pre-tax profit of $2 million.
