## Easy explanation
Inflation happens when prices across stores steadily climb over time, meaning each banknote buys fewer eggs, bread, or bus tickets than before.

## Details
When aggregate demand outpaces productive supply or when input shortages spike manufacturing costs, overall price levels rise. Moderate and predictable inflation encourages consumption and investment today rather than hoarding cash, but runaway inflation destroys savings and strains living standards.

## Technical definition
A macroeconomic condition characterized by a persistent and widespread expansion in consumer price indices, reducing the domestic purchasing unit value of legal currency.

## Examples
If annual inflation is 5 percent, a grocery shopping cart that cost $100 last year requires $105 to purchase the exact same provisions today.
