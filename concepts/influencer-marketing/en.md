## Easy explanation
Instead of shouting through a public loudspeaker in the town square, a business invites a well-respected local food critic to try their specialty dishes and share an honest recommendation with their devoted dining club members.

## Details
Influencer marketing leverages the social capital, credibility, and content production skills of independent digital creators across platforms such as YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram. Creators are categorized across tiers ranging from nano-influencers (under 10,000 followers) to macro- and celebrity tiers. Collaborations include sponsored reviews, product gifting, affiliate links, and co-branded collections. Because consumer trust relies on perceived authenticity, government bodies like the US Federal Trade Commission (FTC) mandate conspicuous disclosures (such as `#ad` or paid partnership tags) whenever a material business relationship exists between a creator and an endorsed brand.

## Technical definition
A strategic commercial collaboration framework in which organizations compensate third-party content producers possessing recognized domain authority or captive audience reach to deliver native commercial endorsements.

## Examples
A sustainable skincare company sends a new organic facial sunscreen to twenty beauty vloggers on YouTube, sponsoring their summer skincare routine videos where they demonstrate application techniques and share a ten percent promotional coupon code.
