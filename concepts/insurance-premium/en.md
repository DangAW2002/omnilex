## Easy explanation
An insurance premium is like a subscription fee for an emergency umbrella: you pay a small fee every month to keep the umbrella ready, so if a sudden storm hits, the insurance company shields you from financial ruin.

## Details
Premiums are determined by actuaries who calculate the mathematical probability and expected financial severity of claims across a pool of policyholders. Factors influencing individual rates include underwriting risk profiles (such as age, driving record, or medical history), chosen coverage limits, and deductible amounts. Policies generally lapse and terminate coverage if scheduled premium payments are missed beyond a standard grace period (typically 30 days). Premiums can be structured as monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, or annual payments.

## Technical definition
The consideration paid by an insured party to an insurer under an insurance contract, transferring specified contingent risk liabilities in exchange for contractual indemnification.

## Examples
A homeowner pays an annual insurance premium of $1,400 to secure $350,000 in comprehensive property replacement coverage against fire, theft, and storm damage.
