## Easy explanation
An interest rate is the cost of renting someone else's money over time.

## Details
Expressed as an annual percentage, interest rates compensate lenders for opportunity costs, inflation risk, and default risk.

## Technical definition
The contractual ratio of interest paid or received to principal balance over a specified measurement epoch.

## Examples
A 5% annual interest rate on a $1,000 balance yields $50 per year before compounding adjustments.
