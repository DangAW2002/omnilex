## Easy explanation
Picture a triangle balanced on its point: the widest part at the top holds the heaviest, most critical facts, while smaller details taper toward the bottom.

## Details
Born during the era of the telegraph when line transmissions could abruptly cut out, the inverted pyramid structure places conclusions first. Journalists assemble stories by ordering information from most critical to least essential. This format allows newspaper layout editors to trim bottom paragraphs without losing narrative coherence and serves time-pressed digital audiences scanning on mobile displays.

## Technical definition
A journalistic organizational structure arranging factual information in descending order of news value, beginning with the decisive outcome.

## Examples
A dispatch on a warehouse fire opens with the casualty count and status of the blaze, moves to the estimated property damages, and closes with the building's historical founding date.
