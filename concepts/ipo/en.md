## Easy explanation
An IPO is the grand debut where a private company opens its doors to the public stock market, allowing anyone with a brokerage account to buy ownership shares.

## Details
During this milestone offering, early venture backers and founders gain market liquidity while the corporation secures substantial growth capital directly from institutional and retail investors. The transition demands strict compliance with regulatory accounting mandates, regular quarterly disclosures, and ongoing board oversight.

## Technical definition
The statutory process whereby an issuer issues common equity securities to the general public for the first time, establishing registration under securities laws.

## Examples
A cloud infrastructure startup sells 15 million shares at $24 per share on Nasdaq, raising $360 million in fresh balance sheet capital.
