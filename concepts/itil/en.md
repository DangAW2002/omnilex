## Easy explanation
ITIL is the standard recipe book for enterprise technology teams. Instead of reinventing how to manage help desks or apply software updates, companies follow ITIL's proven blueprints to avoid common pitfalls.

## Details
Originally established by the UK government and currently governed by Axelos, ITIL provides standardized vocabulary and operational guidelines for technology services. Its practices encompass service catalog management, capacity planning, configuration baselines, and disaster recovery. Modern ITIL 4 integrates agile methodologies, continuous delivery pipelines, and cross-functional value streams to ensure technology directly supports customer value.

## Technical definition
A comprehensive library of prescriptive best practices and procedural standards establishing lifecycle governance, service value chains, and continuous improvement for IT operations.

## Examples
An infrastructure team adopts the ITIL change management guidelines, introducing a change advisory board to review all monthly firewall updates before implementation.
