## Easy explanation
ITSM is running an IT department like a reliable utility provider. Rather than having engineers fix things randomly when something breaks, the team follows clear procedures for launching servers, fixing disruptions, and scheduling updates safely.

## Details
ITSM treats internal technology infrastructure as a portfolio of customer-facing services with formal commitments. Key operational workflows include incident response to restore outages quickly, change enablement to review code updates before production releases, and problem management to eliminate recurring root causes. It bridges everyday technical troubleshooting with strategic business objectives and financial governance.

## Technical definition
A strategic approach to designing, delivering, managing, and improving how information technology is deployed within an enterprise to meet operational service level agreements.

## Examples
An enterprise IT group relies on ITSM workflows to ensure that applying security patches across 200 virtual machines requires impact review and rollback plans before deployment.
