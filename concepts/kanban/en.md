## Easy explanation
Imagine a highway where metered entrance ramps restrict the number of cars on the road at one moment, preventing bumper-to-bumper traffic jams and keeping vehicles cruising smoothly toward their exits.

## Details
Kanban visualizes work on a physical or digital board divided into vertical columns representing stages like "Backlog", "In Development", "Code Review", and "Deployed". Central to the methodology is the enforcement of Work In Progress (WIP) limits on active columns. When a column hits its maximum capacity, no new tasks can enter until existing items move forward. This pull-based mechanism quickly highlights bottlenecks and encourages team members to swarm on blocked tasks.

## Technical definition
A lean agile workflow system that visualizes operations, caps work-in-progress quantities at each station, and optimizes cycle time via continuous value delivery.

## Examples
An IT infrastructure team uses a Kanban board with a strict limit of three simultaneous server migration tickets to prevent engineers from juggling too many tasks at once.
