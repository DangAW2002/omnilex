## Easy explanation
Think of a master professor tutoring an apprentice: instead of forcing the apprentice to read a whole university library from scratch, the master summarizes the key concepts and thought processes, letting the student learn the essentials in a fraction of the time.

## Details
In knowledge distillation, a cumbersome, high-capacity "teacher" model generates predictions over a training corpus. Rather than training the smaller "student" model purely on discrete binary labels (hard targets), the student learns against the teacher's continuous probability outputs (soft targets) using temperature-scaled cross-entropy. These soft distributions reveal rich dark knowledge—such as subtle similarities between non-target classes—allowing the student to retain much of the teacher's accuracy while running vastly faster.

## Technical definition
A supervised model-reduction framework wherein a parameter-constrained student network minimizes divergence between its predictive distribution and the softened logit outputs of a trained teacher network.

## Examples
Distilling a 70B parameter frontier model into an 8B parameter student model that runs smoothly on consumer laptops with minimal drop in response quality.
