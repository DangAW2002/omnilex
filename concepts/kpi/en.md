## Easy explanation
Think of a KPI like the dashboard instruments in your vehicle: the speedometer, fuel gauge, and engine temperature tell you whether the car is cruising safely and efficiently on your journey.

## Details
KPIs provide ongoing visibility into the operational health of products, teams, and enterprises. Effective KPIs adhere to SMART criteria: specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound. Product managers track a balanced scorecard of KPIs spanning acquisition (conversion rates), engagement (active users), retention (churn rates), and monetization (customer lifetime value). Monitoring trends against historical baselines alerts leadership to emerging anomalies before systemic failure occurs.

## Technical definition
A quantifiable measure utilized to assess the continuous operational success of an organization or functional unit in meeting critical strategic objectives.

## Examples
An e-commerce mobile application establishes an operational KPI requiring payment processing checkout latency to remain below 800 milliseconds for 99.9% of transactions.
