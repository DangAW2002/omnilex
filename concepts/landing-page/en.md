## Easy explanation
Unlike a department store's front lobby filled with dozens of doors and escalators, a landing page is like a dedicated boutique pop-up shop built around one single product with a clear checkout counter right in front of you.

## Details
In digital advertising and conversion optimization, a landing page focuses on a singular conversion goal rather than broad exploratory navigation. General homepages feature complex multi-level menus, corporate overviews, and numerous outbound links that can distract prospective customers. In contrast, effective campaign landing pages minimize external navigation, align visual messaging directly with the preceding ad copy, state clear value propositions, and prominently position a single call-to-action button or lead capture form.

## Technical definition
A standalone destination web document rendered upon fulfilling a specific inbound referral URI, architected to minimize decision fatigue and maximize user conversion probability for a defined business campaign.

## Examples
A cloud software company runs Google Search ads for "inventory management software"; clicking the ad takes prospects to a dedicated page offering a 14-day free trial signup form rather than the general company homepage.
