## Easy explanation
A liability is any debt or financial promise that a business owes to someone else, such as bank loans, unpaid supplier invoices, or wages owed to staff.

## Details
Liabilities show where borrowed funds came from to purchase company assets. They are classified into current liabilities due within one operating year, including accounts payable and accrued payroll, and non-current obligations such as 10-year bonds or mortgage debt. Managing debt repayment schedules is critical to preventing technical default.

## Technical definition
A present duty or responsibility to transfer economic resources to another party resulting from past transactions or binding contractual commitments.

## Examples
A construction business carries $300,000 in timber vendor invoices payable in thirty days and a $1.2 million equipment loan payable over five years.
