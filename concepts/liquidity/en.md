## Easy explanation
Liquidity describes how fast you can turn something you own into cash in hand without being forced to offer a desperate bargain discount.

## Details
Cash is the ultimate liquid benchmark asset. Public blue-chip stocks and treasury bills possess deep liquidity because exchange buyers and sellers transact constantly with minimal spreads. Conversely, commercial real estate, private startup stakes, or customized industrial robots are highly illiquid because finding a buyer takes months and rapid disposal demands steep discounts.

## Technical definition
The characteristic of an asset or market measuring the immediacy and low friction with which positions can be converted into legal tender without affecting prevailing market price.

## Examples
Treasury bonds can be converted to cash in seconds on electronic trading venues, whereas selling a commercial office building often requires six months of escrow and inspections.
