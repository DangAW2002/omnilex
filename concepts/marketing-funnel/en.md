## Easy explanation
Picture pouring water into a kitchen funnel: a wide pour enters the broad rim at the top, but only a concentrated, narrower stream flows out of the narrow spout at the bottom.

## Details
The marketing funnel visually maps the sequential stages through which prospective buyers transition during commercial relationships. Classical frameworks (such as AIDA: Awareness, Interest, Desire, Action) divide the pipeline into Top of the Funnel (TOFU - broad brand awareness and educational content), Middle of the Funnel (MOFU - comparison, product evaluation, and lead nurturing), and Bottom of the Funnel (BOFU - specific product demos, transactional conversion, and checkout). Modern digital analytics platforms track micro-steps across this journey, measuring user drop-off rates at every transition stage. By identifying where high abandonment occurs, businesses optimize messaging, reduce friction, and improve customer lifetime value.

## Technical definition
A sequential stage-gate behavioral model and telemetry framework quantifying population attrition across progressive decision milestones along a multi-touch attribution pathway.

## Examples
An online enterprise software vendor attracts 50,000 blog visitors at the top of the funnel, converts 2,000 into trial signups in the middle, and closes 100 enterprise annual contracts at the bottom of the funnel.
