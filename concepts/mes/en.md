## Easy explanation
Like the conductor and backstage coordinator of a theatre production, turning the script written by business executives into real-time cues for actors, props, and lighting crew on the stage floor.

## Details
Positioned at Level 3 of the ANSI/ISA-95 enterprise integration model, a Manufacturing Execution System (MES) bridges business enterprise resource planning (ERP) suites and shop-floor automation controllers. Key functional capabilities include production order scheduling, electronic batch recording, raw material inventory consumption tracking, quality management, and labor allocation. By capturing real-time telemetry from PLCs and barcoding scanners, the MES provides complete digital component genealogy, enforces correct operating procedures, and identifies manufacturing bottlenecks as work orders progress.

## Technical definition
A comprehensive digital manufacturing operations management system that delivers real-time visibility, execution control, material traceability, and automated documentation across shop-floor production workflows.

## Examples
An electronics assembly plant uses an MES to verify that surface-mount component reels match product schematics before surface-mount placement robots begin soldering circuit boards.
