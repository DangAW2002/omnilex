## Easy explanation
Picture giving a machine a digital translator and grammatical reader that extracts the actual sentiment and intent behind words, rather than viewing sentences as meaningless sequences of letters.

## Details
Natural language processing bridges computational logic with human linguistic expression through statistical and neural modeling. Core pipeline stages encompass text tokenization, part-of-speech tagging, lemmatization, named entity recognition, semantic parsing, and topic modeling. Recent paradigms rely on dense vector embeddings generated by large transformer architectures, enabling unified models to execute sentiment analysis, machine translation, and text summarization without bespoke rule engines.

## Technical definition
The multidisciplinary branch of computer science and cognitive artificial intelligence dedicated to the algorithmic parsing, semantic interpretation, and synthesis of human natural languages.

## Examples
A customer feedback platform evaluates 1,000 incoming support tickets, accurately flagging 820 as general billing inquiries and 180 as urgent server outages while extracting user account numbers.
