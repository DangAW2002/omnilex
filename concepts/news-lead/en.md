## Easy explanation
The lead is like an elevator pitch for an entire news story: it answers the biggest questions in a single breath before diving into background details.

## Details
Traditional news leads answer the classic journalistic questions: who, what, when, where, why, and how. By packing the primary facts into the opening sentence, the lead ensures readers grasp the event immediately even if they read no further. While breaking news calls for hard leads delivering direct facts, narrative features often deploy anecdotal leads that introduce a scene or person to pull audiences into a broader investigation.

## Technical definition
The opening sentence or introductory paragraph of a news report that establishes the primary angle and delivers the most consequential factual points.

## Examples
"City council members voted unanimously on Tuesday to approve a new metro line connecting the international airport with downtown districts."
