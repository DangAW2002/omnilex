## Easy explanation
Like a printed neighborhood bulletin or magazine subscription dropped into your physical front porch mailbox every Friday morning, an email newsletter lands directly in your digital inbox packed with stories, news, and tips you signed up to receive.

## Details
An email newsletter represents an owned media communication channel free from third-party algorithmic feed suppression. Sent on a recurring schedule (such as daily, weekly, or monthly), newsletters provide curated commentary, company announcements, educational guides, and exclusive offers. Modern email service providers (ESPs) like Mailchimp enable audience segmentation, dynamic personalization, and responsive HTML template rendering across mobile and desktop email clients. Because legal regulations (such as CAN-SPAM and GDPR) enforce strict subscriber consent, legitimate newsletters require explicit opt-in confirmation and must include a functional one-click unsubscribe mechanism in every email footer.

## Technical definition
A serialized electronic message transmission deployed via Simple Mail Transfer Protocol (SMTP) to a defined list of recipient mailbox endpoints who have registered verified consent.

## Examples
A cybersecurity consultancy sends a weekly digest every Monday morning to ten thousand IT managers, summarizing newly uncovered system vulnerabilities and practical remediation patches.
