## Easy explanation
Think of a North Star metric as Polaris guiding ancient sailors through dark, uncharted seas: regardless of storm swells or wind shifts, keeping that single bright celestial reference point centered guarantees the ship stays on course toward safe harbor.

## Details
Popularized in product growth literature, a North Star metric distills a company's value proposition into one measurable indicator reflecting customer value. Crucially, it balances customer satisfaction against business profitability. If customers extract tangible utility, they return regularly, generating durable revenue. A robust North Star metric is supported by three to five granular "input metrics" spanning breadth (user reach), depth (engagement intensity), frequency (usage recurrence), and efficiency.

## Technical definition
A centralized product metric that serves as the primary leading indicator of sustainable customer value creation and long-term business enterprise health.

## Examples
For a music streaming platform, the North Star metric is "Total weekly hours listened", reflecting both user delight and ad/subscription revenue potential.
