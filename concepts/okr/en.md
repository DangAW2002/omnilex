## Easy explanation
Think of OKRs as training for a mountain marathon: your Objective is to become an elite endurance athlete, while your Key Results are running 40 kilometers every week, dropping resting heart rate to 50 bpm, and finishing a trial race under four hours.

## Details
Popularized at Intel and Google by John Doerr, OKRs align teams around clear priorities over quarterly cadences. An Objective answers "Where do we want to go?", serving as a concise, motivating, qualitative destination. Key Results answer "How do we know we are getting there?", functioning as quantifiable, measurable milestones with starting and target numbers. OKRs are decoupled from direct salary bonuses to foster ambitious stretch thinking without penalizing employees for missing aggressive targets.

## Technical definition
A strategic management framework linking qualitative organizational objectives with a set of quantitative, measurable key results evaluated across specific quarterly intervals.

## Examples
An objective to "Deliver the most trustworthy fintech onboarding in Southeast Asia" is paired with Key Results: reduce account verification time from 24 hours to 5 minutes, and lower fraud rates below 0.01%.
