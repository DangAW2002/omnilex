## Easy explanation
An op-ed is like a guest speaker invited to take the stage: an outside expert writes a persuasive essay sharing their personal take on a contentious issue.

## Details
Named historically for appearing on the page opposite the newspaper's own institutional editorial, the op-ed platform welcomes outside voices. Economists, scientists, legal scholars, and community leaders author these essays to debate public policies, cultural shifts, or moral quandaries. Editorial pages curate diverse perspectives, requiring writers to disclose financial interests or affiliations while substantiating claims with verifiable evidence.

## Technical definition
A signed analytical essay published in a news medium presenting the personal viewpoints of an external author independent of the publication's editorial board.

## Examples
A university climatologist authors an op-ed arguing for municipal investment in coastal sea walls, presenting localized weather data and engineering cost estimates.
