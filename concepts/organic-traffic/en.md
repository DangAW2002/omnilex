## Easy explanation
Think of foot traffic outside a bookstore: people who wander in simply because your display window caught their eye are like organic visitors, while people who step in because a paid street promoter handed them a coupon represent paid traffic.

## Details
In web analytics platforms such as Google Analytics, organic traffic refers specifically to sessions initiated when a user performs a search and clicks an unpaid listing. Unlike paid search campaigns where traffic stops the instant advertising budgets expire, organic traffic builds cumulative value over time as established content maintains authority. Measuring organic traffic involves tracking session volume, active landing pages, keyword impressions, and bounce rates to understand user acquisition trends across search platforms.

## Technical definition
Web user sessions generated via uncompensated algorithmic rankings on search retrieval platforms, categorized within digital analytics attribution models by the medium parameter value of organic search.

## Examples
A personal finance website publishes a comprehensive mortgage calculator; over twelve months, it attracts fifty thousand visitors every month entirely from Google search rankings without any ad spend.
