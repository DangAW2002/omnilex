## Easy explanation
A paywall is like a velvet rope at a theater door: you can see the posters and read the titles in the lobby, but you must buy a ticket before stepping inside to watch the show.

## Details
As print circulations declined and digital advertising revenues shifted toward dominant tech search platforms, publishers turned to subscription paywalls to build durable reader revenues. Implementation models vary: hard paywalls block all non-subscriber access immediately, metered paywalls allow readers a quota of monthly articles before locking, and dynamic paywalls use machine learning to predict which readers are most likely to subscribe based on consumption habits.

## Technical definition
A software gating architecture on digital content platforms that intercepts user requests and restricts full access to articles or media streams unless verified credentials authenticate paid subscription status.

## Examples
A financial news publication allows anonymous visitors to read the introductory two sentences of an investigative market analysis before displaying a overlay prompting them to enter credit card details for a monthly membership.
