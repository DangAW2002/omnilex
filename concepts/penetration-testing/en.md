## Easy explanation
Think of hiring a professional security inspector to attempt breaking into your bank vault: they try picking locks, probing window alarms, and testing employee alertness to give you a detailed report of flaws before real bank robbers arrive.

## Details
Penetration testing (commonly called pentesting) involves certified security practitioners simulating adversarial tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) within agreed-upon Rules of Engagement. Testers progress through reconnaissance, vulnerability scanning, exploitation, lateral movement, and privilege escalation. Unlike automated scanners that merely list theoretical bugs, pentesting actively chains multiple minor flaws together to demonstrate real-world business impact. Findings are compiled into an executive summary and technical remediation roadmap, helping organizations validate defensive postures and satisfy regulatory compliance standards.

## Technical definition
A specialized security testing methodology where authorized assessors launch goal-oriented cyberattacks against infrastructure and software to identify exploitable vulnerabilities and evaluate defense efficacy.

## Examples
A fintech startup hires an external offensive security firm to conduct a two-week grey-box penetration test against its core banking APIs before launching its consumer mobile application.
