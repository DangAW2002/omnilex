## Easy explanation
Like paying annual community dues to maintain common neighborhood facilities, personal income tax is a contribution deducted from your earnings to fund public roads, schools, hospitals, and emergency services.

## Details
Most sovereign jurisdictions operate progressive tax systems where higher tiers of taxable earnings face incrementally steeper marginal rates. Taxable income is calculated by taking gross worldwide earnings and subtracting statutory adjustments, deductions, and personal exemptions. Employers frequently withhold estimated taxes directly from paychecks throughout the year. Taxpayers subsequently file an annual reconciliation return to determine whether they owe additional liabilities or qualify for a refund.

## Technical definition
A statutory direct tax imposed upon the comprehensive net financial income accrued by natural persons within an assessment period, calculated according to established progressive or flat rate schedules.

## Examples
An employee with $80,000 in gross annual salary takes a $14,600 standard deduction, leaving $65,400 in taxable income distributed across the 10%, 12%, and 22% federal tax brackets.
