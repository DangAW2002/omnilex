## Easy explanation
A podcast is like on-demand radio tailored to your favorite topics: you subscribe to a show on your phone and fresh episodes download automatically for listening during a commute or workout.

## Details
Originally emerging from the convergence of iPod hardware and RSS feed syndication, podcasts encompass talk shows, narrative audio dramas, investigative journalism, and educational deep-dives. Creators host audio files on dedicated servers and generate an RSS feed containing episode metadata. Podcasting applications check these feeds regularly, enabling decentralized distribution free from algorithmic curation or platform exclusivity.

## Technical definition
A decentralized digital media syndication protocol utilizing RSS enclosures to distribute episodic audio recordings over computer networks for automated retrieval and local playback.

## Examples
A financial analytics firm produces a weekly forty-minute podcast interviewing startup founders, distributing episodes via Apple Podcasts and Spotify feeds to thousands of commuting executives.
