## Easy explanation
A POS system is the digital cash register at a store counter. When a cashier scans your items, the screen tallies the bill, accepts payment, prints a paper receipt, and updates inventory stock behind the scenes.

## Details
POS solutions pair physical hardware—barcode scanners, cash drawers, touchscreens, and card terminals—with transaction software. Beyond taking money, they record sales taxes, apply promotional discounts, issue refunds, and adjust store inventory in real time. Modern cloud POS terminals can also run on tablets, allowing retail staff to process orders and take card payments anywhere on the sales floor.

## Technical definition
A localized transaction processing terminal combining physical peripheral interfaces with transactional software to validate commercial checkouts, process tender, and synchronize catalog inventory.

## Examples
A coffee shop cashier taps an espresso on a touchscreen POS, scans a loyalty barcode from the customer's phone, and settles the order via contactless bank card.
