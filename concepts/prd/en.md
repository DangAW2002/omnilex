## Easy explanation
Think of a PRD as a detailed brief given to an architect: it specifies that the building needs four bedrooms, natural light in the kitchen, and solar panels on the roof, guiding the builders on what to construct without telling them which brand of hammer to swing.

## Details
A PRD synthesizes user research, strategic objectives, and operational parameters into an actionable blueprint. Core components include the problem statement, user personas, workflow journeys, functional requirements, technical dependencies, and target success metrics. Rather than serving as an inflexible contractual decree, a contemporary agile PRD functions as a living document updated dynamically as prototypes undergo testing and technical feasibility studies unearth new realities.

## Technical definition
A formal product management artifact that establishes the functional scope, behavioral specifications, user interactions, and validation criteria for an engineering capability.

## Examples
A product manager writes a PRD for an in-app tipping feature, specifying payment flow interfaces, settlement limits, tax receipt compliance, and real-time push notifications for recipients.
