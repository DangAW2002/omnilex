## Easy explanation
A press conference is like an open town hall for reporters: leaders step up to microphones on a stage, read an important announcement, and answer tough questions from the crowd.

## Details
Press conferences allow public officials and corporate leaders to broadcast unified announcements simultaneously to television networks, wire agencies, and newspapers. The format typically opens with prepared remarks before the floor is opened for questions moderated by a communications officer. High-stakes conferences require careful rehearsal, technical audio-visual setups for broadcast cameras, and background press kits distributed upon arrival.

## Technical definition
A structured public relations event where media representatives are assembled to receive official statements and conduct unscripted question-and-answer exchanges with institutional figures.

## Examples
Following a major hurricane, the regional disaster management commissioner convenes a live press conference at command headquarters to update citizens on evacuation routes and emergency shelter capacities.
