## Easy explanation
An embargo is like getting an early invitation to an unboxing party: you receive the gadget beforehand so you can inspect it thoroughly, but you promise not to post your review online until the clock strikes the agreed hour.

## Details
Embargoes serve both public relations teams and newsrooms by providing journalists ample time to conduct interviews, digest dense reports, and craft nuanced stories before public release. In exchange for advanced access, participating outlets commit to hold publication until the lifting time. Leaking early violates journalistic trust, prompting organizations to blacklist offending reporters from future advance briefings.

## Technical definition
A voluntary convention in media relations where source materials are distributed ahead of an event on condition that reports remain unpublished until a predetermined date and hour.

## Examples
A pharmaceutical firm shares clinical trial results with medical correspondents on Monday morning under an embargo expiring at noon on Thursday, aligning with publication in a peer-reviewed journal.
