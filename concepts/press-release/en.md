## Easy explanation
A press release is like a formal written memo sent to a newsroom: an organization writes down its big news clearly so reporters have all the facts in one handy document.

## Details
Press releases follow a standardized news format featuring a clear headline, publication date, dateline, lead paragraph, quote from leadership, and a corporate boilerplate summarizing the issuing entity. They deliver factual announcements regarding quarterly financial earnings, executive changes, product debuts, or public policy updates. Journalists treat releases as raw tips and institutional positions, independently investigating facts rather than republishing text verbatim.

## Technical definition
An official written document drafted by a publicist or media relations officer and circulated to journalistic outlets to announce newsworthy developments.

## Examples
An automaker issues a two-page press release announcing a billion-dollar electric vehicle manufacturing facility, providing technical specs and quote attributions from the chief executive.
