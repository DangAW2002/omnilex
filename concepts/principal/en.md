## Easy explanation
Principal is the original money you put in or borrow, like the initial seed before any financial fruit grows.

## Details
In lending and investing, principal serves as the basis for interest calculation. Over time, payments may reduce this initial principal balance.

## Technical definition
The primary capital sum placed into an investment or owed on a debt instrument, distinct from interest, dividends, or fees.

## Examples
If you deposit $1,000 into a savings account, that $1,000 is your principal.
