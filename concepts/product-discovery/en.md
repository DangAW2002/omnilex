## Easy explanation
Think of product discovery as prospectors drilling small core samples across a hillside to confirm the presence of gold veins before spending millions transporting heavy excavators and building a commercial mine.

## Details
Product discovery runs in tandem with engineering delivery to ensure teams build the right thing rather than merely building things right. Product managers, UX designers, and lead engineers conduct customer interviews, study usage analytics, and deploy rapid interactive prototypes. Discovery rigorously confronts four core risks: value risk (will customers buy it?), usability risk (can users figure out how to use it?), feasibility risk (can our engineers build it?), and business viability risk (does it work for legal, finance, and sales?).

## Technical definition
The continuous, evidence-based process undertaken by cross-functional product teams to rapidly separate good product ideas from flawed concepts prior to engineering commitment.

## Examples
Before creating an AI scheduling tool, a team spends three weeks interviewing 40 executive assistants and observing how they manually coordinate calendar invitations across time zones.
