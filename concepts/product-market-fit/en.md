## Easy explanation
Think of product-market fit as launching a food truck serving hot, delicious soup on a freezing winter sidewalk: customers form lines around the corner, demand overwhelms your capacity, and people eagerly tell their coworkers to visit.

## Details
Coined in the venture startup ecosystem, product-market fit marks the tipping point where a company transitions from speculative experimentation to repeatable growth. It requires being in a viable market with a product capable of satisfying that market. Quantitative indicators include flat retention curves where a cohort of users remains active indefinitely, strong organic word-of-mouth referral rates, and survey metrics where over 40% of users state they would be "very disappointed" if the product disappeared tomorrow.

## Technical definition
The state of operational equilibrium where a company's value proposition and feature capabilities directly fulfill widespread, demonstrable demand in a sustainable addressable market.

## Examples
A messaging collaboration application experiences explosive organic adoption as tech companies invite external clients, who in turn adopt the software across their own internal departments.
