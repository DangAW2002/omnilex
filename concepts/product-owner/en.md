## Easy explanation
Think of a product owner as the film director who decides which scenes make the final cut to tell a compelling story, balancing actor ideas and producer budgets while keeping audiences enthralled.

## Details
The product owner represents customer desires and business objectives directly within the Scrum team. Their primary mandate is optimizing the return on investment of engineering effort. The PO crafts the long-term product goal, drafts user stories, clarifies requirements, and establishes backlog sequencing. While the PO gathers input from users, sales executives, and technical architects, they hold individual accountability for decisions on feature prioritization.

## Technical definition
The Scrum team role answerable for how much value the developers' work produces, which includes owning and ordering the product backlog.

## Examples
A product owner for a logistics application interviews delivery drivers, identifies GPS routing delays, and prioritizes offline map capabilities above all other initiatives in the upcoming sprint.
