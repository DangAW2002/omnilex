## Easy explanation
Think of a product roadmap as an architect's master plan for a multi-year campus build: it outlines when the foundation, library, and sports arena will open over the coming years without prescribing which brand of doorknob to install today.

## Details
A product roadmap aligns stakeholders across the business around shared strategic priorities. Modern roadmaps frequently organize deliverables around horizons like "Now / Next / Later" or problem-focused themes rather than strict calendar deadlines. This format prevents engineering teams from making fragile long-range delivery promises while preserving agility to pivot as new user data surfaces. Roadmaps communicate the business rationale behind engineering choices to marketing, sales, and executives.

## Technical definition
A high-level visual artifact articulating a product's strategic objectives, major milestone initiatives, and anticipated evolution across upcoming business quarters.

## Examples
A SaaS company's roadmap indicates that Q1 focuses on enterprise single sign-on security, Q2 tackles automated billing reconciliation, and Q3 explores AI document extraction.
