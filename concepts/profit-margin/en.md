## Easy explanation
Profit margin shows how much profit a company retains from each dollar of revenue, using a stated measure of profit.

## Details
There are several profit margins. Gross margin divides gross profit by revenue, where gross profit is revenue less the cost of goods sold. Other margins use profit figures after additional expenses.

## Technical definition
Profit margin is the ratio of a specified profit measure to the corresponding revenue, usually expressed as a percentage. The type of profit must be stated for a meaningful comparison.

## Examples
Revenue of $100 and cost of goods sold of $60 produce gross profit of $40 and a gross margin of 40%.
