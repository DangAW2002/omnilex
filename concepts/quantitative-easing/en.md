## Easy explanation
When interest rates are already zero and cannot be dropped further, quantitative easing is how central banks create electronic money to purchase bonds and pump cash into the financial system.

## Details
The central bank credits commercial bank reserves while buying up long-dated government gilts or mortgage securities. This large-scale asset purchase program drives bond prices upward, lowering long-term bond yields and encouraging institutional funds to lend to businesses and households.

## Technical definition
An extraordinary monetary policy operation consisting of non-sterilized open-market purchases of longer-duration securities to expand bank reserves and reduce long-term term premia.

## Examples
The Federal Reserve purchases $80 billion in US Treasury bonds monthly to suppress long-term mortgage rates during severe macroeconomic distress.
