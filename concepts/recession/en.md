## Easy explanation
A recession is a prolonged economic winter where factories make fewer items, stores sell less merchandise, and many workers lose their jobs.

## Details
While popular commentary frequently cites two consecutive quarters of negative real GDP growth as the working rule of thumb, formal determination committees examine employment levels, retail sales volumes, and industrial production data. Recessions form normal phases of the macroeconomic business cycle that purge economic imbalances.

## Technical definition
A recurring phase of the business cycle characterized by a substantial, broad-based decline in real output, real income, employment, and industrial production.

## Examples
Following banking panics, real national output shrinks for three straight quarters while corporate layoffs push national unemployment up from 4 to 9 percent.
