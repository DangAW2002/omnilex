## Easy explanation
Imagine a customer browsing jackets in your boutique who walks out without purchasing; later that afternoon, when they stop at a café down the street, your store assistant politely hands them a voucher for that exact jacket.

## Details
Retargeting functions by identifying prospective buyers who interacted with digital touchpoints but failed to complete a desired conversion action. Traditionally executed using browser tracking pixels or device advertising identifiers, modern platforms rely increasingly on first-party data segments and server-side tagging. Advertisers define custom audience segments based on visit recency, viewed product categories, or cart abandonment. When these individuals browse partner websites across ad networks or social feeds, specialized bidding algorithms deliver personalized ad creatives designed to reactivate consideration and overcome purchase hesitation.

## Technical definition
A programmatic advertising delivery mechanism that re-engages qualified user profiles identified by prior behavioral event telemetry through deterministic identity graph matching or first-party cookie segments across third-party display networks.

## Examples
A traveler views a beach resort in Da Nang on a booking portal but leaves the site before reserving; over the next three days, banner ads displaying special room discounts for that specific resort appear while the user reads articles on local news websites.
