## Easy explanation
Think of retention rate like a local gym's New Year membership drive: out of one hundred people who sign up in January, the retention rate counts how many dedicated members are still showing up regularly to lift weights in June.

## Details
Retention rate is the ultimate barometer of authentic product value; without retention, user acquisition is unsustainable. The formula measures customers active at the end of a period minus newly acquired customers, divided by initial customers at period start. Product teams visualize retention through cohort analysis curves tracking cohorts over Day 1, Day 7, Day 30, and Day 90. When the retention curve flattens horizontally rather than decaying toward zero, the product possesses a loyal core base.

## Technical definition
The proportion of an existing customer cohort that maintains an active, ongoing commercial or operational relationship with a product over an assessed time period.

## Examples
A language learning mobile app discovers that 45% of users who install the application complete lessons on Day 30, signaling an exceptional retention benchmark in the consumer education space.
