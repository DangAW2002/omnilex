## Easy explanation
Revenue represents the top line of business income, tallying up every dollar paid or promised by clients for goods delivered and services rendered.

## Details
Often designated as gross turnover, revenue forms the baseline starting point for performance evaluation. Modern accounting frameworks require performance obligations to be satisfied before revenue is recognized, regardless of when invoice cash actually arrives. Growth in top-line figures must be balanced against operating margins to ensure sustainability.

## Technical definition
The gross economic inflow of economic benefits arising in the course of ordinary operating activities that results in an increase in equity.

## Examples
A coffee roaster delivers 1,000 bags of specialty beans at $20 each, booking $20,000 in top-line sales revenue for that shipment.
