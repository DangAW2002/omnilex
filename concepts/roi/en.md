## Easy explanation
For every dollar spent, how many dollars of net profit came back into your pocket.

## Details
Calculated as (Net Profit / Cost of Investment) * 100%. Widely used in capital allocation decisions and performance marketing evaluations.

## Technical definition
A financial metric expressed as a percentage that assesses the return on capital relative to initial asset expenditure.

## Examples
Spending $5,000 on infrastructure yielding $15,000 in operational savings results in a 200% ROI.
