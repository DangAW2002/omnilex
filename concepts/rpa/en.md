## Easy explanation
RPA is like having a digital ghost worker sitting at a computer. Instead of a clerk spending three hours copying data from invoices and typing it into an old accounting software, an RPA bot opens the windows, clicks the fields, and enters the data automatically.

## Details
RPA platforms record user interactions with desktop and browser applications, replaying those steps across repetitive administrative tasks. Bots extract text, validate field values, populate forms, and transfer data between legacy applications that lack modern APIs. Implementations range from attended bots that assist human operators during customer calls to unattended bots that run in scheduled background batches on virtual machines.

## Technical definition
A software automation mechanism that executes scripted procedural workflows by programmatically capturing and driving graphical user interface controls and object models across disparate business applications.

## Examples
An insurance firm deploys unattended RPA bots to log into a state motor registry website every midnight, download accident claims, and paste the policy numbers into an internal verification database.
