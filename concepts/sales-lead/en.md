## Easy explanation
When someone walks into a car dealership showroom, asks detailed questions about an electric vehicle, and leaves their phone number and email address with a sales representative for a callback, they have become a lead.

## Details
In customer relationship management (CRM) and digital marketing funnels, a lead represents an unverified potential buyer who entered the sales pipeline by submitting contact credentials. Organizations collect leads through lead capture forms, gated whitepapers, webinar registrations, trade shows, and inbound telephone inquiries. Marketing automation systems evaluate incoming leads through lead scoring—a framework assessing demographic fit and behavioral signals (such as visiting pricing pages or opening promotional emails). Leads typically progress from raw contacts to Marketing Qualified Leads (MQLs), and upon validation of budget, authority, need, and timeline (BANT criteria), transition to Sales Qualified Leads (SQLs) for direct representative outreach.

## Technical definition
A qualified entity record in a customer relationship management schema representing an identifiable person or corporate contact who has demonstrated measurable commercial intent.

## Examples
A manufacturing firm downloads an industrial solar feasibility study from a renewable energy provider's website, submitting an operations manager's email and company phone number to receive the technical PDF.
