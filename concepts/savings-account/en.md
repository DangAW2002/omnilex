## Easy explanation
A savings account is like a secure digital piggy bank at the bank: your coins are kept locked away from daily spending impulses, and the bank rewards your patience by adding a small interest bonus over time.

## Details
Depositors open savings accounts to earn interest on funds not required for immediate transactions. In the United States, eligible accounts receive statutory insurance up to $250,000 per depositor per insured institution by the FDIC. Interest rates fluctuate based on benchmark monetary policy set by central banks. While funds remain liquid and withdrawable on demand, banks discourage using savings accounts for frequent routine shopping payments, reserving transaction privileges for checking accounts.

## Technical definition
A retail demand deposit liability held on a depository institution's balance sheet that accrues interest periodically and allows immediate withdrawal without penalty or advance notice.

## Examples
An account holder deposits $10,000 in an online high-yield savings account yielding 4.2% annual percentage yield (APY), generating $420 in compounded interest over one year while remaining fully accessible.
