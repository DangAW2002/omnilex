## Easy explanation
Think of scope creep as packing for a weekend camping trip: you start with a tent and sleeping bag, but then casually toss in folding chairs, a portable espresso machine, a kayak, and a generator until your car trunk refuses to shut and departure is delayed by hours.

## Details
Scope creep takes root when informal promises, casual stakeholder requests, or architectural perfectionism sneak into active work streams without formal impact assessments. In software engineering, this manifests as developers casually tacking unrequested capabilities onto existing user stories or executives requesting "just one tiny extra dropdown." Over time, these unmanaged additions exhaust developer capacity, erode test coverage, inflate technical debt, and push delivery dates far beyond committed deadlines.

## Technical definition
The uncontrolled expansion of deliverables or requirements in a software project occurring when changes are incorporated without formal evaluation of time, budget, or resource impacts.

## Examples
A simple user profile redesign spirals into scope creep when stakeholders demand a social feed, follower mechanisms, and customized avatar generators within the same delivery deadline.
