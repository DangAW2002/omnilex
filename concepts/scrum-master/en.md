## Easy explanation
Think of a scrum master as a sports referee and athletic coach combined: ensuring players adhere to the rules, smoothing disputes, and clearing hazards off the field so athletes can perform at their highest potential.

## Details
The scrum master acts as a servant leader for the Scrum squad and the broader organization. They do not manage personnel or assign tasks; rather, they foster psychological safety, facilitate ceremonies, and shield engineers from distracting corporate overhead. When developers encounter blockers—such as delayed API access, hardware deficits, or unclear dependencies—the scrum master actively negotiates solutions with external departments to clear the path.

## Technical definition
The role in the Scrum team accountable for establishing Scrum according to the Scrum Guide and assisting the team in improving productivity and self-management.

## Examples
When a front-end engineering squad is stalled waiting for external cloud database credentials, the scrum master liaises directly with infrastructure leads to expedite clearance.
