## Easy explanation
Imagine a basketball squad holding quick timeouts between plays to review tactics, adapt to court dynamics, and execute coordinated drills rather than executing a rigid plan conceived weeks earlier.

## Details
Scrum organizes work around autonomous, cross-functional squads containing developers, a product owner, and a scrum master. Work advances in predictable iterations termed sprints, typically ranging from one to four weeks. Every cycle begins with collaborative sprint planning, tracks daily trajectory via fifteen-minute syncs, inspects finished output during a sprint review, and refines internal operational mechanics during a retrospective. Transparency, empirical inspection, and continuous adaptation serve as its foundation.

## Technical definition
An empirical framework for agile product delivery enabling cross-functional teams to deliver valuable increments iteratively through self-management and formal inspect-and-adapt feedback loops.

## Examples
A payments platform team uses two-week Scrum iterations to introduce QR-code settlement capabilities, reviewing progress with merchants at each sprint conclusion.
