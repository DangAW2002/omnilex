## Easy explanation
When you walk into a hardware store and say "cordless drill", that phrase immediately guides the shopkeeper to the right aisle: in search engines, a keyword serves as that exact request directing algorithms to matching shelves of information.

## Details
Search engines evaluate textual queries to understand what a searcher intends to achieve, categorized broadly into informational, navigational, commercial, or transactional intent. Content creators optimize pages by aligning headings, body paragraphs, and descriptive metadata with primary keywords and related semantically connected phrases. Modern search algorithms evaluate context, synonyms, and natural topic coverage rather than strict exact-match keyword density, penalizing repetitive keyword stuffing that harms readability.

## Technical definition
A lexical token or phrase utilized within search queries and indexed by search algorithms to compute thematic relevance between user retrieval requests and document corpora.

## Examples
A boutique bakery in Austin targets the keyword "gluten-free wedding cakes Austin" so couples looking for specialized catering discover their portfolio directly in local search results.
