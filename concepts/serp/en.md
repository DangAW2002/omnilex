## Easy explanation
When you ask an inquiry desk for recommendations, the clerk hands you a single sheet of paper listing top matches, highlighted bullet points, maps, and special sponsor notices: that sheet is the SERP.

## Details
A search engine results page displays multiple categories of information tailored to user intent. Traditional organic results consist of title links, breadcrumb navigation, and descriptive snippets summarizing the target webpage. Alongside standard blue links, modern SERPs incorporate rich results generated from structured data, interactive carousels, local map packs, image grids, and direct answer modules. Position zero features and generative AI summaries frequently satisfy query intent directly on the page, reshaping how organic traffic flows to publishers.

## Technical definition
The dynamic document interface rendered by an information retrieval system that aggregates ranked organic citations, sponsored listings, and structured knowledge components corresponding to an inputted query token sequence.

## Examples
Searching "how to change a flat tire" produces a results page featuring a step-by-step video carousel at the top, a local roadside assistance map, and ten organic blue links from automotive websites.
