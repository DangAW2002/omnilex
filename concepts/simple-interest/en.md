## Easy explanation
Simple interest pays you the exact same flat fee every period based on what you started with.

## Details
Calculated using the formula I = P * r * t. Because accrued interest is never reinvested, returns grow linearly rather than exponentially.

## Technical definition
Non-capitalizing interest calculated strictly upon original principal for the duration of the contractual tenure.

## Examples
A $10,000 bond paying 5% simple annual interest produces $500 each year regardless of holding period.
