## Easy explanation
Like a modern office tower that senses when employees arrive in the morning, automatically waking up elevators, dialing in comfortable desk lighting, and lowering air conditioning in conference rooms only when meetings are actually taking place.

## Details
Referenced in building automation specifications and international standards such as ITU-T Y.4409, a smart building integrates disparate mechanical, electrical, and plumbing subsystems into a cohesive Building Management System (BMS). Using protocols like BACnet, Modbus, and IP networks, the facility coordinates Variable Air Volume (VAV) cooling, hydronic heating valves, dynamic window shading, smart elevators, and badge security gates. Integrated environmental sensors track indoor CO2 concentrations, ambient luminosity, and acoustic noise, dynamically modulating HVAC fans to preserve indoor air comfort while minimizing kilowatt-hour consumption.

## Technical definition
An architectural facility equipped with automated sensing, building management networks, and computational control systems that dynamically harmonize environmental comfort, resource efficiency, and facility safety.

## Examples
A corporate headquarters tower uses ceiling-mounted presence sensors and daylight harvesting light fixtures across twenty floors to reduce HVAC fan speeds in unoccupied wings, lowering overall building energy utility bills by thirty percent.
