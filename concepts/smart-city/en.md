## Easy explanation
Like a modern metropolis equipped with digital senses, where streetlights dim when streets are empty, traffic signals automatically adjust to ease traffic jams, and trash cans text sanitation trucks when they are full.

## Details
Defined by ITU-T Recommendation Y.4201, a smart city utilizes horizontal IoT platforms to connect disparate vertical municipal domains. Embedded sensors monitor road traffic density, ambient air quality (PM2.5, NO2), electrical grid distribution, stormwater sewer levels, and public waste receptacles. These telemetry streams converge into municipal data platforms, feeding automated actuation workflows and decision-support dashboards. Public benefits include reduced carbon footprints, faster emergency dispatch routing, predictive infrastructure repairs, and transparent citizen communication portals.

## Technical definition
An innovative urban municipality leveraging advanced information and telecommunication infrastructures and analytical systems to improve operational efficiency, urban sustainability, and citizen quality of life.

## Examples
A coastal municipality deploys acoustic stormwater pipe monitors, smart LED streetlights, and coordinated adaptive traffic signals, cutting municipal street lighting electricity expenditures by forty percent and reducing peak traffic congestion by twenty-five percent.
