## Easy explanation
A smart home is a living space where everyday items like light switches, air conditioners, and window curtains communicate over a wireless network, allowing the house to automatically turn off lights when you leave or turn on air conditioning before you arrive.

## Details
A smart home architecture comprises end devices (smart plugs, thermostats, presence detectors), local controllers or hubs, and optional cloud automation engines. Communication commonly relies on localized wireless standards (Thread, Zigbee, Z-Wave, BLE, Wi-Fi) managed via smartphone applications or voice assistants. The unified Matter standard provides application-layer interoperability across Apple Home, Google Home, and Amazon Alexa. Security considerations involve local cryptographic key storage, segmented home Wi-Fi VLANs, and edge automation execution to safeguard homeowner privacy.

## Technical definition
An automated residential dwelling infrastructure consisting of networked sensors, actuators, and computing controllers cooperating via wireless protocols to automate domestic processes and resource management.

## Examples
A smart home routine that dims the living room lamps, lowers motorized window blinds, and sets the air conditioner to 24 degrees Celsius when a motion sensor detects movie playback on the television.
