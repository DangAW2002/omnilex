## Easy explanation
Think of a municipal emergency dispatch headquarters: dispatchers and response coordinators sit before wall-to-wall monitors 24 hours a day, triaging incoming emergency phone calls, dispatching police or firefighters to incidents, and coordinating crisis recovery.

## Details
A Security Operations Center (SOC) brings together people, processes, and technology to defend an enterprise against cyber threats around the clock. SOC operations are usually structured in tiers: Tier 1 triage analysts review incoming alerts from SIEM and EDR platforms; Tier 2 incident responders perform deep-dive forensics and threat containment; and Tier 3 threat hunters proactively search for stealthy adversaries. Standard operating procedures dictate strict containment playbooks, incident escalation pathways, digital evidence preservation, and post-incident root cause analysis.

## Technical definition
A centralized organizational unit comprising personnel, technology, and governance processes tasked with continuous situational awareness, telemetry monitoring, incident triage, and cyber threat mitigation.

## Examples
A Tier 1 analyst in a corporate SOC triages an endpoint telemetry alert at 2:00 AM, confirms an unauthorized PowerShell download script, and isolates the affected workstation from the corporate network within three minutes.
