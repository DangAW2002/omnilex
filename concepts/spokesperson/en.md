## Easy explanation
A spokesperson is the official voice of an organization: like an ambassador, whatever they say represents the entire company, not just their personal opinion.

## Details
Spokespeople bridge internal executive decision-making and external public scrutiny. They participate in media training to deliver consistent core messages, clarify technical complexities, and withstand aggressive investigative questioning. Beyond formal podium briefings, spokespeople build professional relationships with industry correspondents, fielding daily inquiries and shaping organizational narratives across print and broadcast channels.

## Technical definition
An authorized individual appointed by an institution to communicate its official positions, policies, and actions to the media and the public.

## Examples
The ministry of foreign affairs spokesperson steps up to the podium during a weekly media briefing to state the government's formal diplomatic stance on cross-border trade negotiations.
