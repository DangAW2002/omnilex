## Easy explanation
Think of sprint planning as mapping out an expedition route before leaving base camp: the group agrees on the summit target, packs only the supplies they can carry, and charts daily stages.

## Details
Sprint planning kicks off every iteration and lasts at most eight hours when the sprint is one month long; shorter sprints get a shorter planning session. The gathering addresses three key topics: Why is this sprint valuable (formulating the sprint goal)? What can be done in this sprint (selecting backlog items)? How will the team deliver it (decomposing items into technical tasks)? The resulting plan forms the sprint backlog.

## Technical definition
The initial formal event of a sprint wherein the entire Scrum team agrees on a sprint goal together and selects actionable product backlog items based on capacity.

## Examples
During a three-hour planning meeting, an engineering team commits to eight user stories required to support a new credit card gateway, establishing a clear sprint goal for launch.
