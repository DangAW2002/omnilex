## Easy explanation
Think of a sprint retrospective as a post-race team debrief in professional motorsports: engineers and drivers review pit-stop delays, analyze tire wear, and tune engine protocols before the next race.

## Details
The retrospective closes the active sprint, occurring right after the sprint review. It focuses strictly on internal operations rather than product features. Participants inspect how the iteration unfolded with respect to individuals, interactions, workflows, and environmental tooling. The team identifies what worked well, what caused friction, and what should change. The highest-priority actionable improvement items are often integrated directly into the upcoming sprint backlog.

## Technical definition
A timeboxed inspect-and-adapt ceremony concluding a sprint, where the Scrum team decides how to raise software quality and operational effectiveness.

## Examples
Following a messy release caused by failing automated tests, the team uses its retrospective to agree on a rule that all pull requests must execute smoke tests before merging.
