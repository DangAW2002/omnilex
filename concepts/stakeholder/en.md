## Easy explanation
Think of stakeholders as everyone seated around a community town hall table when a new railway is proposed: passengers want punctuality, local residents worry about noise, city planners track safety, and taxpayers scrutinize the budget.

## Details
Stakeholders represent diverse internal and external constituencies with a vested interest in the product's trajectory. Internal stakeholders include executive sponsors, sales representatives, customer support leads, compliance attorneys, and security teams. External stakeholders encompass paying clients, regulatory bodies, and supply partners. Effective product managers maintain proactive communication cadences with each cohort to identify hidden operational constraints and secure buy-in without letting design-by-committee derail the roadmap.

## Technical definition
An individual, group, or organization that holds a legitimate stake, requirement, or interest in the operational life cycle or outcomes of a system.

## Examples
During an enterprise software overhaul, stakeholders include the chief security officer requiring strict audit logs and the customer support manager advocating for faster ticket-search tools.
