## Easy explanation
Pre-money valuation is the negotiated price tag placed on a startup before a new investor writes a check and deposits money into its bank account.

## Details
The figure is crucial because adding the new cash investment to the pre-money valuation yields the post-money valuation, which directly sets the percentage stake the incoming investor receives. Founders strive for higher pre-money valuations to minimize dilution, while investors balance valuation against sustainable future milestones.

## Technical definition
The negotiated enterprise valuation of an entity prior to the receipt of proceeds from a primary equity issuance, establishing share price on a fully diluted basis.

## Examples
An entrepreneur agrees to an $8 million pre-money valuation with a venture fund investing $2 million, creating a $10 million post-money value with the investor owning 20 percent.
