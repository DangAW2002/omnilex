## Easy explanation
A storyboard is like a comic strip of your movie drawn on paper before you shoot it: it helps the entire crew picture every camera angle and action before turning on the expensive gear.

## Details
Pre-production relies on storyboards to translate abstract screenplay directions into concrete visual language. Each drawn panel depicts frame composition, camera movement arrows (pans, tilts, zooms), character blocking, and dialogue cues. In animated films and visual effects heavy action movies, storyboards are scanned and edited with scratch audio into timed video reels called animatics to test pacing and ensure spatial continuity.

## Technical definition
A graphic organizer displaying illustrations or images arranged chronologically to pre-visualize motion picture sequences, interactive media, or animations.

## Examples
A television commercial director presents a twelve-frame storyboard to an advertising client, demonstrating how the product reveal transitions smoothly into the concluding brand logo animation.
