## Easy explanation
Think of using flight simulator software to train airline pilots in dangerous thunderstorms, rather than having to wait for a real storm and putting real passengers at risk.

## Details
Synthetic data generation addresses data scarcity, licensing constraints, and privacy regulations like GDPR and HIPAA. Techniques range from physics-based 3D graphics rendering engines (producing labeled driving simulations) to generative models like GANs and diffusion architectures that learn real empirical distributions. Synthetic data expands corner-case representation and allows cross-border machine learning experimentation without exposing protected private records.

## Technical definition
Information generated through computational simulations or algorithmic sampling that preserves target marginal and joint probability distributions without using actual empirical records.

## Examples
A medical research institute manufactures 50,000 synthetic patient health records with realistic blood pressure metrics to test an analytics platform without exposing real patient identities.
