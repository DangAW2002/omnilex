## Easy explanation
Like an aircraft flight data recorder plugged directly into a commercial van or delivery truck, continuously radioing speed, sudden braking, fuel mileage, and engine warning lights back to the dispatcher's headquarters.

## Details
Telematics unifies satellite navigation, mobile cellular connectivity, and vehicular on-board computing. Telematics Control Units (TCUs) interface directly with vehicle CAN buses via standard diagnostic interfaces like OBD-II for passenger vehicles or SAE J1939 for heavy commercial trucks. Internal three-axis accelerometers measure harsh braking, rapid acceleration, and cornering lateral G-forces. This combined diagnostic and behavioral stream is uploaded over 4G/5G links, enabling logistics fleets to detect aggressive driving habits, reconstruct crash events, schedule engine maintenance, and reduce insurance risk.

## Technical definition
The integrated branch of engineering and information science encompassing the collection, transmission, and computation of vehicular operational metrics, positional data, and safety telemetry across wide-area wireless networks.

## Examples
A national delivery fleet installs telematics control units across five thousand courier vans to track fuel consumption, optimize delivery routing, and monitor driver braking behavior, lowering monthly fuel expenditure by twelve percent.
