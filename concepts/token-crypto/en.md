## Easy explanation
Like arcade tokens: you convert regular currency into custom tokens usable only within specific decentralized game systems or platforms.

## Details
Unlike native network coins (such as BTC or ETH), tokens rely on underlying smart contract standards such as ERC-20 or SPL on Solana.

## Technical definition
A fungible or non-fungible programmable cryptographic ledger entry administered by smart contracts on a distributed ledger.

## Examples
The UNI governance token or the USDC stablecoin smart contract deployed on the Ethereum blockchain.
