## Easy explanation
AI does not read complete words like people do; it chops words into Lego-like bricks called tokens to calculate next-word probabilities.

## Details
In English, 1,000 tokens equal roughly 750 words. Model context limits and API pricing tiers are measured strictly in token counts.

## Technical definition
The atomic sequence unit generated by a tokenizer (e.g. BPE) fed into the transformer embedding layer.

## Examples
The word "unbelievable" might be split into tokens: ["un", "believ", "able"].
