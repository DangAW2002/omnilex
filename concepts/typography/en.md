## Easy explanation
Typography is like the clothing that words wear: a formal serif font looks like a tailored business suit, while a bubbly rounded font looks like casual vacation attire.

## Details
Good typography balances aesthetic character with effortless readability. Designers manipulate typeface classifications (serif, sans-serif, slab, monospace, display), hierarchy scales, tracking (letter spacing across whole words), kerning (space between individual character pairs), leading (line height), and line length (measure). In digital user interfaces, responsive typography adjusts fluidly across smartphone screens and desktop monitors to prevent eye strain.

## Technical definition
The art, process, and technical discipline of designing, selecting, and arranging letterforms, typefaces, and whitespace within graphic compositions to optimize visual communication and legibility.

## Examples
A luxury watch catalog pairs a classical high-contrast serif typeface for elegant chapter headings with a clean geometric sans-serif for tiny technical movement specifications.
