## Easy explanation
Think of a user story as an index card stating a customer wish list item in plain language, describing who wants the tool and what problem it solves without getting tangled in engineering jargon.

## Details
User stories commonly follow a standardized narrative template: "As a [type of user], I want [an action] so that [a benefit is achieved]." This structure ensures engineers remain anchored to genuine customer motivations rather than abstract technical chores. Stories serve as invitations to collaborative conversations between product managers, designers, and developers. Each story carries acceptance criteria stating when the requirement has been successfully realized.

## Technical definition
A lightweight functional requirement artifact in agile development defining a discrete slice of end-user functionality and its associated business rationale.

## Examples
"As a shopper on an e-commerce platform, I want to keep products in a saved list so that I can watch for price drops and purchase them on payday."
