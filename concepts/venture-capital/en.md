## Easy explanation
Venture capital is funding provided by professional investors to ambitious young startups that carry high risk of failure but have the potential to grow massive.

## Details
Venture firms raise pooled capital from institutional limited partners to finance emerging enterprises across progressive funding stages from Seed through Series A, B, and C. Because the vast majority of startups fail, fund economics depend heavily on rare breakout winners that return the entire fund through massive acquisitions or public IPOs.

## Technical definition
Private equity institutional capital pooled into limited partnerships to acquire minority equity stakes in unquoted early-stage enterprises exhibiting asymmetric risk-reward profiles.

## Examples
A Silicon Valley venture fund invests $5 million into an AI drug discovery startup in return for a 20 percent preferred equity ownership position.
