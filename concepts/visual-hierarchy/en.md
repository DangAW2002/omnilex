## Easy explanation
Visual hierarchy is like organizing an orchestra: the brass section blasts the main melody so you notice it first, while the softer flutes and violins provide supporting background harmony without competing.

## Details
Graphic designers establish visual hierarchy through scale, contrast, color saturation, proximity, and spatial alignment. By sizing dominant headlines substantially larger than secondary subheads and body copy, layouts signal where human attention should land first. Strategic use of white space isolates critical call-to-action buttons, preventing clutter and enabling readers to scan dense information architectures quickly.

## Technical definition
The deliberate spatial and aesthetic arrangement of graphical elements within a visual field to communicate relative importance and direct the sequential path of visual perception.

## Examples
A newspaper front page establishes hierarchy with a huge seventy-two-point top headline, a medium twenty-four-point secondary deck, and small ten-point three-column body text accented by bold pull quotes.
