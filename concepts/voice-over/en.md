## Easy explanation
A voice-over is like a tour guide talking in your ear while you look at museum exhibits: you hear their clear voice explaining the sights, but their face never shows up in the picture.

## Details
Voice-over techniques feature extensively in documentaries, commercials, instructional tutorials, audiobooks, and video games. Voice actors record scripted narration inside isolated sound booths equipped with sensitive condenser microphones and pop filters. Audio engineers process the recorded tracks through equalization, compression, and de-essing before ducking background music tracks so speech remains effortlessly legible.

## Technical definition
An audio production technique where an off-camera narrator or actor provides recorded spoken dialogue that accompanies visual cinematography without showing the speaker's physical mouth movements.

## Examples
A nature documentary presents slow-motion footage of a cheetah stalking prey across the savannah while an unseen narrator calmly whispers the predator's tactical maneuvers.
