## Easy explanation
WCAG is the international building code for websites: just as civic inspectors require certain stair rail heights and fire exits, WCAG specifies contrast rules and text requirements for digital products.

## Details
Published by the W3C, WCAG is organized around four fundamental tenets: Perceivable, Operable, Understandable, and Robust (POUR). Each guideline features measurable success criteria graded across three conformance tiers: Level A (essential minimum), Level AA (standard target for legal and commercial compliance), and Level AAA (specialized high accessibility). It covers color contrast ratios, focus order, alternative text, and interactive timeouts.

## Technical definition
A standardized technical recommendation by the W3C defining normative testable success criteria for evaluating web accessibility conformance.

## Examples
WCAG 2.2 criterion 1.4.3 requires a minimum color contrast ratio of 4.5:1 for standard body text against background shades at Level AA conformance.
