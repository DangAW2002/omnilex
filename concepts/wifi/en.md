## Easy explanation
Think of an invisible Ethernet cable reaching across your home or office, allowing laptops, smart speakers, and cameras to download movies and stream data through the air without tripping over physical wires.

## Details
Wi-Fi is the commercial brand name certified by the Wi-Fi Alliance for wireless local area network (WLAN) products based on the IEEE 802.11 standards family. Devices broadcast across unlicenced 2.4 GHz, 5 GHz, and 6 GHz (Wi-Fi 6E/7) radio frequency spectrums. Modern generations utilize advanced digital modulation schemes like Orthogonal Frequency Division Multiple Access (OFDMA) and multi-user MIMO to achieve speeds ranging from dozens of megabits up to multiple gigabits per second. In IoT, Wi-Fi is the primary connectivity choice for mains-powered devices requiring continuous cloud bandwidth, such as security cameras and smart appliances.

## Technical definition
A standardized wireless local area networking technology governed by the IEEE 802.11 specifications that facilitates half-duplex radio-frequency data communications across license-exempt radio bands using CSMA/CA channel access.

## Examples
A wireless home security camera transmits a continuous 1080p high-definition video stream over a 5 GHz 802.11ac Wi-Fi connection to a cloud recording server at 4 Mbps.
