## Easy explanation
Working capital is the everyday pocket money of a business, calculated by taking all cash, inventory, and pending customer checks and subtracting bills due within the year.

## Details
Positive net working capital indicates that an organization can pay suppliers, meet payroll, and navigate supplier delays without resorting to emergency credit lines. Efficient working capital management targets quick inventory turnover and prompt customer collections while negotiating reasonable payment terms with suppliers.

## Technical definition
A liquidity metric equal to current assets minus current liabilities, quantifying short-term operational reserves and financial flexibility.

## Examples
A retail grocery store maintains $4 million in cash and store merchandise against $2.5 million in supplier invoices, holding $1.5 million in net working capital.
