# Termbridge Master Index (en)

- [**Compound Interest**](../../concepts/compound-interest/en.md) [finance/personal-finance/saving, finance/investing/portfolio-risk]: *interest on interest, money snowballing because earnings get added back to the pile* — Interest calculated on both the initial principal and the accumulated interest from previous periods.
- [**Cookie**](../../concepts/cookie/en.md) [it/web/frontend, media/digital-marketing/paid-ads]: *small browser file websites save to remember your login, web tracker banner you click accept on* — A small piece of data stored on the user's browser by websites to retain state and track browsing activity.
- [**Interest Rate**](../../concepts/interest-rate/en.md) [finance/economics/interest-rates-monetary-policy, finance/banking/deposits]: *percentage fee charged for borrowing money, percentage reward earned on savings per year* — The proportion of an amount loaned which a lender charges as interest to the borrower, typically expressed as an annual percentage.
- [**Margin**](../../concepts/margin/en.md) [finance/investing/stocks]: *using assets in a brokerage account as collateral to borrow money for securities, the equity required in an account when buying securities with borrowed money* — Assets in a brokerage account support a loan used to buy securities.
- [**Principal**](../../concepts/principal/en.md) [finance/personal-finance/saving, finance/banking/lending]: *original sum of money borrowed or invested, money before any interest gets calculated* — The original sum of money lent, invested, or borrowed before interest accrues.
- [**Profit margin**](../../concepts/profit-margin/en.md) [finance/corporate-finance/financial-statements]: *how much profit a company keeps from each dollar of revenue, the percentage of sales that remains as profit* — A percentage comparing a specified measure of profit with the corresponding revenue.
- [**Return on Investment**](../../concepts/roi/en.md) [finance/investing/valuation, media/digital-marketing/metrics-analytics]: *how much money you make back compared to what you spent, performance metric measuring profitability of an expense* — A performance metric used to evaluate the efficiency or profitability of an investment relative to its cost.
- [**Simple Interest**](../../concepts/simple-interest/en.md) [finance/personal-finance/saving, finance/banking/lending]: *interest calculated solely on the initial deposit, fixed interest payment that never compounds* — Interest calculated exclusively on the original principal amount for the entirety of the loan or deposit period.
- [**Token (Authentication)**](../../concepts/token-auth/en.md) [it/security/authentication, it/web/api]: *digital access pass sent with web requests, encoded string proving you are logged in* — A cryptographically signed string presented to servers to prove identity and authorization permissions.
- [**Token (Cryptocurrency)**](../../concepts/token-crypto/en.md) [finance/investing/digital-assets]: *digital asset issued on an existing blockchain, utility or governance coin within a decentralized app* — A digital unit of value issued on top of an existing smart contract blockchain platform.
- [**Token (AI)**](../../concepts/token-llm/en.md) [it/ai-ml/llm-agents]: *unit AI uses to count text chunks, why AI charges per prompt length* — Small chunk of text, typically subword units, processed by large language models.
