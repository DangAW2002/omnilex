# Termbridge Index: Finance (en)

- [**Accrual accounting**](../../concepts/accrual-accounting/en.md): *accounting standard recording sales when earned and costs when incurred, not when cash changes hands; financial reporting principle linking revenues with related expenses regardless of payment dates* — The financial reporting method recording revenues when earned and expenses when incurred, regardless of the timing of cash receipts or disbursements.
- [**Asset (Accounting)**](../../concepts/asset/en.md): *anything of economic value owned or controlled by a person or company; valuable resource expected to generate future financial benefits or cash* — A present economic resource controlled by an entity as a result of past events from which future economic benefits are expected to flow.
- [**Balance sheet**](../../concepts/balance-sheet/en.md): *financial snapshot showing what a business owns and owes at one moment; document balancing company assets against debts and owner equity* — A core financial statement summarizing an entity’s assets, liabilities, and shareholders’ equity at a specific point in time.
- [**Bank transfer**](../../concepts/bank-transfer/en.md): *electronic moving of money from one bank account directly into another; digital payment sent between bank accounts without physical cash or paper checks* — An electronic transfer of funds from one bank account to another across an interbank clearing and settlement network.
- [**Blockchain**](../../concepts/blockchain/en.md): *a shared digital record book where transactions are grouped into blocks and locked together cryptographically; a decentralized ledger maintained by a network of computers rather than a central server or bank* — A decentralized, tamper-resistant digital ledger of transactions recorded cryptographically in linked chronological blocks across a distributed network.
- [**Bond (Finance)**](../../concepts/bond/en.md): *loan you make to a government or corporation that pays you fixed interest and returns your money at maturity; fixed-income investment where an issuer promises regular interest payments and full principal repayment* — A fixed-income debt instrument issued by governments or corporations that pays periodic interest to bondholders and repays principal at maturity.
- [**Yield (Finance)**](../../concepts/bond-yield/en.md): *percentage return earned annually on a bond based on its current market price; effective interest rate generated by a bond investment over time* — The percentage rate of return generated on a bond or fixed-income investment relative to its purchase price or par value.
- [**Budget (Personal finance)**](../../concepts/budget/en.md): *plan for how much money to spend and save each month; roadmap tracking incoming paychecks against upcoming bills* — An itemised financial plan that estimates anticipated income and allocates it toward expenses, savings, and debt payoff over a set period.
- [**Cash flow statement**](../../concepts/cash-flow-statement/en.md): *document tracking hard currency entering and leaving corporate bank accounts; financial report breaking down cash changes into operating, investing, and financing* — A financial statement tracking the aggregate inflows and outflows of actual cash across operating, investing, and financing operations.
- [**Compound Interest**](../../concepts/compound-interest/en.md): *interest on interest; money snowballing because earnings get added back to the pile* — Interest calculated on both the initial principal and the accumulated interest from previous periods.
- [**Compound Interest**](../../concepts/compound-interest/en.md): *interest on interest; money snowballing because earnings get added back to the pile* — Interest calculated on both the initial principal and the accumulated interest from previous periods.
- [**Consumer Price Index (CPI)**](../../concepts/cpi/en.md): *statistical basket tracking price changes for everyday goods bought by households; standard economic benchmark measuring the cost of living fluctuations* — A statistical index measuring the weighted average change over time in prices paid by households for a representative basket of consumer goods and services.
- [**Credit card**](../../concepts/credit-card/en.md): *payment card that lets you buy things on credit up to a preset limit; plastic card allowing you to borrow money from a bank for purchases and pay later* — A payment card issued by a financial institution that enables cardholders to borrow funds up to an approved limit to make purchases or cash advances.
- [**Credit score**](../../concepts/credit-score/en.md): *three-digit number showing lenders the probability of repaying borrowed money on time; personal financial grade determining whether you get approved for loans and credit cards* — A numerical assessment calculated from credit history that indicates an individual's creditworthiness and likelihood of repaying debt obligations on time.
- [**Cryptocurrency**](../../concepts/cryptocurrency/en.md): *digital money that uses cryptography instead of a central bank to secure transactions and control creation; electronic currency exchanged directly between people on a decentralized computer network* — A digital or virtual currency that relies on cryptographic mechanisms for security and operates across a decentralized distributed ledger.
- [**Debt-to-equity ratio (D/E)**](../../concepts/debt-to-equity-ratio/en.md): *proportion comparing how much a company borrows against how much shareholders own; financial leverage yardstick measuring debt dollars per dollar of net equity* — A financial leverage metric calculated by dividing an entity’s total debt obligations by its shareholders’ equity.
- [**Deflation**](../../concepts/deflation/en.md): *ongoing drop in general retail prices across an entire economy; economic condition where goods get cheaper but debts become harder to repay* — A persistent and widespread decline in the general price level of goods and services across an economy.
- [**Depreciation (Accounting)**](../../concepts/depreciation-accounting/en.md): *spreading out the purchase cost of heavy equipment over the years it gets used; accounting write-down reflecting wear, tear, and aging of physical assets* — The systematic allocation of the depreciable cost of a tangible fixed asset over its estimated useful economic lifespan.
- [**Diversification**](../../concepts/diversification/en.md): *spreading your investment money across many different assets so a loss in one will not wipe you out; the practice of not putting all your eggs into a single investment basket* — A risk-management technique that mixes a wide variety of investments within a portfolio to reduce unsystematic risk.
- [**Dividend**](../../concepts/dividend/en.md): *portion of company profits distributed directly to shareholders; cash payout received per share for owning stock in a profitable company* — A distribution of a portion of a company's post-tax earnings decided by the board of directors and paid out to shareholders.
- [**Double-entry bookkeeping**](../../concepts/double-entry-bookkeeping/en.md): *accounting rule requiring every single financial transaction to be recorded in at least two accounts; system matching debits against credits to keep financial balance sheets in equilibrium* — The universal bookkeeping method wherein every economic transaction is recorded in at least two separate ledger accounts with offsetting debit and credit entries.
- [**E-wallet**](../../concepts/e-wallet/en.md): *smartphone app that stores payment details to pay for things online or in stores; electronic account holding stored balance or linked cards for contactless and online checkouts* — An electronic application or device that securely stores digital payment credentials and funds to facilitate online, in-app, and contactless transactions.
- [**EBITDA**](../../concepts/ebitda/en.md): *profit gauge ignoring interest payments, tax bills, and asset wear and tear; operational cash earnings metric stripping out financing and accounting choices* — An earnings metric measuring core operating profitability before interest, tax, depreciation, and amortization deductions.
- [**Emergency fund**](../../concepts/emergency-fund/en.md): *cash set aside to cover unexpected life emergencies without borrowing; safety net of three to six months of living expenses kept in a bank* — A liquid cash reserve maintained specifically to absorb unforeseen expenses or income disruptions without taking on debt or liquidating long-term investments.
- [**Equity (Finance)**](../../concepts/equity-finance/en.md): *value left for owners after paying off all business liabilities; total stake held by shareholders including contributed capital and accumulated profits* — The residual economic interest in an entity’s assets after deducting all of its liabilities.
- [**Exchange-Traded Fund (ETF)**](../../concepts/etf/en.md): *an investment fund traded on a stock exchange like an individual stock throughout the trading day; a basket of securities that you can buy and sell instantly with fluctuating market prices* — An investment fund traded directly on securities exchanges that holds a portfolio of assets designed to track an underlying index or sector.
- [**Exchange rate**](../../concepts/exchange-rate/en.md): *relative price tag determining how much foreign currency you get for one unit of domestic money; conversion value used when exchanging one nation’s currency for another* — The comparative price of one national currency expressed in terms of another currency.
- [**Gross Domestic Product (GDP)**](../../concepts/gdp/en.md): *total monetary value of all finished goods and services made within a country during a year; master scoreboard measuring the overall size and health of a nation’s economy* — The total monetary or market value of all final goods and services produced within a country’s domestic borders over a specific timeframe.
- [**International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS)**](../../concepts/ifrs/en.md): *global rulebook ensuring company financial statements look consistent across different countries; international accounting standards bringing transparency and comparability to corporate reports* — A globally recognized framework of principles-based reporting rules established by IASB authorities to unify corporate accounts.
- [**Income statement**](../../concepts/income-statement/en.md): *scorecard showing money earned and spent by a company over a duration; financial report demonstrating net profit or loss during a quarter or year* — A financial report illustrating a company’s operational revenues, expenses, and resultant net earnings over a specified period.
- [**Index fund**](../../concepts/index-fund/en.md): *an investment fund designed to automatically mirror the performance of a specific market index; a low-fee fund that buys all the stocks in a benchmark index instead of trying to pick individual winners* — A passively managed mutual fund or exchange-traded fund designed to replicate the performance and asset weighting of a specific benchmark financial market index.
- [**Inflation**](../../concepts/inflation/en.md): *general rise in prices across an economy that makes money lose purchasing power; situation where your cash buys fewer groceries and goods than it did previously* — A broad and sustained increase in the general price level of goods and services over time, eroding currency purchasing power.
- [**Deductible (Insurance)**](../../concepts/insurance-deductible/en.md): *amount of money you must pay yourself before insurance starts covering expenses; upfront out-of-pocket cost required before your insurer pays a claim* — The specified amount of out-of-pocket expenses that an insured policyholder must pay toward covered losses before the insurer begins paying benefits.
- [**Insurance premium**](../../concepts/insurance-premium/en.md): *regular payment you make to an insurance company to keep your coverage active; monthly or annual price paid for an insurance policy protecting against risks* — The recurring amount of money an individual or business pays to an insurance company to maintain an active insurance policy.
- [**Interest Rate**](../../concepts/interest-rate/en.md): *percentage fee charged for borrowing money; percentage reward earned on savings per year* — The proportion of an amount loaned which a lender charges as interest to the borrower, typically expressed as an annual percentage.
- [**Interest Rate**](../../concepts/interest-rate/en.md): *percentage fee charged for borrowing money; percentage reward earned on savings per year* — The proportion of an amount loaned which a lender charges as interest to the borrower, typically expressed as an annual percentage.
- [**Initial Public Offering (IPO)**](../../concepts/ipo/en.md): *first time a private company sells its shares to everyday public investors; corporate transition when a privately held startup lists on an exchange* — The inaugural public sale of corporate stock on a securities exchange, transforming a private company into a publicly traded entity.
- [**Liability (Accounting)**](../../concepts/liability/en.md): *financial debt or legal obligation owed by an individual or business to outside parties; money that must be paid back to lenders, vendors, or the government over time* — A present financial obligation of an enterprise arising from past events, settlement of which requires an outflow of economic resources.
- [**Liquidity (Finance)**](../../concepts/liquidity/en.md): *ease and speed with which an asset can be converted into ready cash without losing value; ability of an individual or company to pay off immediate debts on time* — The operational ease and velocity with which an economic asset can be converted into spendable currency without suffering significant price erosion.
- [**Margin (Trading)**](../../concepts/margin/en.md): *using assets in a brokerage account as collateral to borrow money for securities; the equity required in an account when buying securities with borrowed money* — Assets in a brokerage account support a loan used to buy securities.
- [**Market capitalization**](../../concepts/market-capitalization/en.md): *the total dollar value of all of a company's outstanding shares of stock on the open market; how much money it would cost to buy every single share of a company at its current price* — The aggregate dollar market value of a publicly traded company's outstanding equity shares calculated by multiplying share count by share price.
- [**Mortgage**](../../concepts/mortgage/en.md): *long-term loan used to buy a home or real estate, secured by the property itself; bank loan where the house serves as collateral if you fail to repay* — A secured loan used to purchase or refinance real estate, where the property serves as collateral until the debt is fully amortized.
