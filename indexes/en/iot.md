# Termbridge Index: Internet of Things (en)

- [**Accelerometer**](../../concepts/accelerometer/en.md): *sensor that detects motion, shakes, and the pull of gravity on a device; microchip that measures changes in speed and tells a phone to rotate its screen* — An electromechanical sensor that measures proper acceleration—the physical acceleration experienced by an object relative to freefall, including the gravitational vector.
- [**Actuator**](../../concepts/actuator/en.md): *mechanism that converts electrical control commands into physical motion; component that moves or controls a physical mechanism in response to a signal* — An output device that converts electrical control signals into physical movement, force, or mechanical displacement.
- [**Analog-to-digital converter (ADC)**](../../concepts/adc/en.md): *electronic circuit that converts smooth continuous physical voltages into discrete digital numbers; sensor-reading chip that turns real-world voltage levels into numbers a computer can process* — An electronic circuit system that samples a continuous analog voltage waveform and quantizes it into discrete multi-bit digital numeric values.
- [**Arduino**](../../concepts/arduino/en.md): *open-source microcontroller board and software platform for controlling physical electronics; easy-to-program electronic prototyping board that reads buttons and turns on motors* — An open-source electronics prototyping platform encompassing programmable microcontroller hardware boards and a simplified software development environment for controlling physical actuators and sensors.
- [**Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE)**](../../concepts/ble/en.md): *short range wireless personal area network technology designed for minimal battery drain; power efficient radio specification for exchanging sensor data with nearby devices* — A power-optimized wireless personal area network technology designed for short-range communication with minimal electrical energy consumption.
- [**Constrained Application Protocol (CoAP)**](../../concepts/coap/en.md): *web transfer protocol tailored for constrained nodes and low bandwidth networks using udp; lightweight http alternative designed to run restful requests on tiny microcontrollers* — A specialized RESTful web transfer protocol designed by the IETF for resource-constrained microcontrollers and low-power wireless networks using UDP.
- [**Device provisioning**](../../concepts/device-provisioning/en.md): *process of enrolling configuring and registering a new hardware unit into a cloud service; initial automated onboarding workflow that assigns credentials and configurations to devices* — The automated onboarding process that authenticates, registers, and configures newly deployed hardware devices into a centralized cloud management platform.
- [**Digital twin**](../../concepts/digital-twin/en.md): *virtual software counterpart of a physical asset updated with real time sensor metrics; computational model reflecting the live state behavior and lifecycle of real world machines* — A virtual representation that serves as the real-time computational counterpart of a physical object, process, or system synchronized through continuous telemetry.
- [**Edge computing**](../../concepts/edge-computing/en.md): *computing paradigm that analyzes and processes information near the data source location; decentralized framework running analytics directly on local hardware instead of remote servers* — A distributed computing paradigm that brings computational logic and data storage closer to the physical devices and sensors where information is generated.
- [**Embedded system**](../../concepts/embedded-system/en.md): *specialized computer built inside a larger machine to run dedicated tasks; compact microprocessor hardware dedicated entirely to single-purpose equipment control* — A dedicated computing assembly combining a microprocessor, firmware, and peripheral interfaces engineered to execute predefined control operations within an encompassing machine.
- [**Energy harvesting**](../../concepts/energy-harvesting/en.md): *capturing ambient energy from light, heat, or vibrations to power electronics without batteries; technology that scavenges electricity from the environment for maintenance-free sensors* — The process of capturing minute quantities of energy from ambient environmental sources—such as light, thermal gradients, mechanical vibrations, or radio waves—and converting it into electrical power for electronics.
- [**ESP32**](../../concepts/esp32/en.md): *powerful, low-cost microchip with built-in Wi-Fi and Bluetooth for connected devices; dual-core microcontroller chip engineered specifically for wireless IoT gadgets* — A low-cost, low-power system on a chip microcontroller series featuring integrated Wi-Fi and dual-mode Bluetooth designed for wireless IoT product engineering.
- [**Firmware**](../../concepts/firmware/en.md): *permanent low level software programmed directly into device non volatile storage; foundational code that instructs hardware components how to communicate and operate* — Low-level software programmed directly into non-volatile memory that provides operational instructions and hardware abstraction for electronic devices.
- [**GNSS**](../../concepts/gnss/en.md): *umbrella term for satellite constellations like GPS, Galileo, and BeiDou that provide global positioning; satellite navigation technology that calculates exact latitude, longitude, and time anywhere on Earth* — The worldwide collection of satellite constellations and augmentation systems that transmit radio signals used by ground receivers to compute autonomous geospatial positioning and atomic time.
- [**General-purpose input/output (GPIO)**](../../concepts/gpio/en.md): *physical pins on a microchip that software can toggle on, off, or read as digital signals; programmable electrical pins used to communicate with switches, LEDs, and external circuits* — An uncommitted digital signal pin on an integrated circuit whose electrical direction and voltage state can be configured dynamically by software at runtime.
- [**I²C**](../../concepts/i2c/en.md): *two-wire communication bus for connecting multiple sensors to a microchip; serial data protocol using a clock line and a data line with unique device addresses* — A synchronous, multi-controller, multi-target serial communication bus that uses two bidirectional open-drain lines (SCL and SDA) pulled high by resistors to network peripheral ICs.
- [**Inertial measurement unit (IMU)**](../../concepts/imu/en.md): *sensor pack combining an accelerometer and gyroscope to track orientation and movement; electronic motion sensor that measures how fast a device is tilting, turning, and accelerating* — An integrated multi-axis sensor package combining accelerometers, gyroscopes, and frequently magnetometers to measure a body linear acceleration, angular velocity, and heading.
- [**Gateway (IoT)**](../../concepts/iot-gateway/en.md): *bridge unit connecting field sensors with external cloud networks and translating protocols; physical hub that aggregates local device signals and forwards filtered payloads upstream* — A bridging hardware device or software appliance that aggregates field sensor data, translates communication protocols, and securely routes telemetry to cloud networks.
- [**IoT platform**](../../concepts/iot-platform/en.md): *cloud or server suite that manages connects and analyzes fleets of connected hardware; central software environment handling communication security and data processing for iot fleets* — A multi-layered middleware software suite that provides connectivity, fleet device management, data ingestion, and application integration for IoT deployments.
- [**LiDAR**](../../concepts/lidar/en.md): *sensor that shoots laser beams and times reflections to create accurate 3D maps; laser-scanning distance measurement system used in self-driving vehicles and robotic vacuums* — A remote-sensing method that illuminates a target with pulsed laser light and measures reflected return times to generate high-resolution three-dimensional spatial coordinates.
- [**LoRa (IoT)**](../../concepts/lora-radio/en.md): *long-distance, low-power radio modulation that sends small sensor messages across kilometers; chirp-based wireless technology that enables battery-operated sensors to transmit data across cities* — A proprietary physical-layer wireless radio frequency modulation technique derived from Chirp Spread Spectrum (CSS) that enables long-range, low-power data communications across license-free sub-gigahertz bands.
- [**LoRaWAN**](../../concepts/lorawan/en.md): *long range low power wireless networking protocol for remote sensor deployments; wide area communication standard that connects battery powered iot devices over kilometers* — A point-to-multipoint Low-Power Wide-Area Network (LPWAN) protocol designed for wireless, battery-operated devices communicating over long distances on unlicensed radio spectrum.
- [**Sleep mode (Embedded)**](../../concepts/low-power-mode/en.md): *power-saving state where a microchip shuts down most circuits to preserve battery life; standby mode that drops microchip power consumption from milliamps to microamps* — A reduced-consumption operational state in microcontrollers where clock oscillators, high-speed peripherals, and processing cores are gated off until an external interrupt or timer triggers awakening.
- [**Matter (IoT)**](../../concepts/matter-iot/en.md): *unified smart home standard that lets Apple, Google, and Amazon devices work seamlessly together; universal language for smart lights, locks, and plugs across different smart home ecosystems* — An open-source, interoperable smart home connectivity standard operating over IP that allows certified devices to communicate seamlessly across Apple Home, Google Home, and Amazon Alexa ecosystems.
- [**Mesh network**](../../concepts/mesh-network/en.md): *network topology where nodes interconnect directly to dynamically relay data for each other; decentralized layout where each participant acts as a forwarding router without a single failure point* — A decentralized network topology where interconnected nodes collaborate to dynamically relay data across multiple hops without relying on a single central hub.
- [**Microcontroller (MCU)**](../../concepts/microcontroller/en.md): *compact single chip computer designed to govern dedicated hardware tasks; integrated circuit containing a processor core memory and input output peripherals* — A compact integrated circuit containing a processor core, memory, and programmable input/output peripherals designed for embedded control operations.
- [**Modbus**](../../concepts/modbus/en.md): *de facto standard industrial protocol that queries memory registers using master client polling; simple serial and tcp communication format widely implemented across manufacturing equipment* — A de facto industrial request-response communication protocol that reads and writes discrete memory coils and 16-bit numerical registers across serial and Ethernet links.
- [**MQTT**](../../concepts/mqtt/en.md): *lightweight messaging protocol using a publish subscribe broker model for iot devices; network standard for sending small data packets between sensors and servers over tcp* — A lightweight publish-subscribe network messaging protocol running over TCP, designed for constrained hardware and low-bandwidth connections.
- [**NB-IoT**](../../concepts/nb-iot/en.md): *cellular radio standard optimized for low bandwidth high density iot device deployments; licensed telecom network specification enabling deep indoor coverage for battery powered meters* — A narrowband cellular radio technology standard developed by 3GPP, designed for massive machine-type communication on licensed telecommunication frequencies.
- [**Near-field communication (NFC)**](../../concepts/nfc/en.md): *short-range wireless touch technology used for contactless payments and transit taps; close-contact wireless standard that transfers credentials when devices are tapped together* — A short-range, high-frequency standards-based wireless connectivity technology operating at 13.56 MHz that enables intuitive two-way communication when devices are brought within 4 centimeters.
- [**OPC UA**](../../concepts/opc-ua/en.md): *cross platform industrial communication architecture providing semantic data modeling and secure transport; machine to machine standard ensuring interoperability between manufacturing hardware across vendors* — A platform-independent, service-oriented communication architecture designed for industrial automation that provides rich semantic information modeling and built-in cryptographic security.
- [**Over-the-air update (OTA)**](../../concepts/ota-update/en.md): *remote wireless delivery of firmware and software updates to connected hardware fleets; process of patching device software over network connections without physical physical contact* — The remote wireless distribution and installation of new software or firmware updates to connected hardware without physical tethering.
- [**Programmable Logic Controller (PLC)**](../../concepts/plc/en.md): *ruggedized industrial computer built to execute deterministic automated factory workflows; hardened controller that reads field sensors and governs machinery through programmed logic* — A ruggedized industrial digital computer designed to execute deterministic, real-time control logic over manufacturing machinery and industrial processes.
- [**Predictive maintenance**](../../concepts/predictive-maintenance/en.md): *maintenance technique using sensor telemetry and analytics to forecast equipment failures before they happen; strategy that schedules machine servicing according to real time condition metrics rather than fixed calendar intervals* — A proactive asset management strategy that continuously monitors operational sensor telemetry to forecast machinery degradation and schedule servicing before mechanical failure occurs.
- [**Pulse-width modulation (PWM)**](../../concepts/pwm/en.md): *rapidly switching a digital signal on and off to simulate variable electrical power; flickering a voltage line fast enough to dim lights or adjust electric motor speeds* — A modulation method that regulates the effective average power delivered to a load by varying the duty cycle of a rapid square-wave switching signal.
- [**Raspberry Pi**](../../concepts/raspberry-pi/en.md): *popular series of small, affordable single-board computers for learning and prototyping; credit-card-sized computer board featuring 40 GPIO pins used widely in DIY and IoT builds* — A widely adopted series of compact, cost-effective ARM-based single-board computers equipped with a standard 40-pin header for peripheral interfacing and education.
- [**Radio-frequency identification (RFID)**](../../concepts/rfid/en.md): *wireless technology using radio waves to identify and track tags attached to objects; electronic tagging system that scans toll transponders and inventory tags from meters away* — A wireless identification technology employing electromagnetic radio frequency fields to automatically detect and read encoded data stored on passive or active electronic tags.
- [**Real-time operating system (RTOS)**](../../concepts/rtos/en.md): *operating system engineered to process events within guaranteed timing constraints; lightweight system that schedules tasks with deterministic latency for embedded hardware* — An operating system designed to execute processes with deterministic timing guarantees and minimal scheduling latency for mission-critical embedded devices.
- [**SCADA**](../../concepts/scada/en.md): *system architecture combining software and hardware to monitor and control expansive industrial facilities; supervisory control setup displaying plant telemetry graphics and handling operator commands* — An industrial control system architecture that gathers real-time telemetry from geographically dispersed field controllers, visualizes operational state, and allows supervisory commands.
- [**Secure boot**](../../concepts/secure-boot/en.md): *security check that ensures a computer or chip only boots trusted, authorized software; cryptographic startup process that stops malicious firmware from running on a device* — A security standard ensuring that an electronic device boots using only firmware and software that is verified and cryptographically signed by trusted manufacturers.
- [**Sensor**](../../concepts/sensor/en.md): *electronic part that detects physical changes like temperature or motion; hardware module that converts real world signals into digital data* — An input hardware component that senses physical attributes of the environment and converts them into measurable electrical signals.
- [**Single-board computer (SBC)**](../../concepts/single-board-computer/en.md): *complete computer built entirely onto one single circuit board; compact circuit board containing processor, memory, and ports capable of booting an OS* — A complete functional computer whose microprocessor, system memory, storage interfaces, input-output controllers, and networking ports are assembled onto a single physical printed circuit board.
- [**Smart home**](../../concepts/smart-home/en.md): *residence equipped with connected devices that automate lighting climate and security; networked household setup where appliances communicate and can be managed remotely* — A residential environment equipped with interconnected electronic devices that automate domestic tasks, optimize utility usage, and enable centralized remote monitoring.
- [**Smart meter**](../../concepts/smart-meter/en.md): *digital utility meter that measures electricity, water, or gas and sends readings automatically; connected electric meter that reports consumption to the power company without manual meter readers* — An advanced electronic utility metering device that records consumption of electric energy, water, or gas in real time and communicates intervals back to the utility provider via automated networks.
- [**Serial Peripheral Interface (SPI)**](../../concepts/spi-bus/en.md): *high-speed, four-wire data connection between a microchip and external memory or displays; synchronous serial communication interface using separate lines for sending and receiving data simultaneously* — A high-speed, synchronous four-wire serial interface providing full-duplex data transfer between a master device and one or more selected peripheral devices using dedicated chip select lines.
- [**Telemetry**](../../concepts/telemetry/en.md): *automated collection and remote transmission of sensor measurements to a monitoring station; continuous stream of operational readings emitted by field hardware across a network* — The automated process of collecting operational metrics and status measurements from remote points and transmitting them across communications channels for monitoring and analysis.
- [**Thread (IoT)**](../../concepts/thread-iot/en.md): *secure, self-healing wireless mesh networking standard designed for smart home gadgets; IPv6-based mesh radio protocol that lets home devices talk to each other without single failure points* — An IPv6-based, low-power wireless mesh networking protocol built on IEEE 802.15.4 that provides reliable, secure, and self-healing connectivity for smart home devices without requiring a central hub.
- [**TinyML**](../../concepts/tinyml/en.md): *running artificial intelligence models directly on tiny, low-power microcontrollers; smart machine learning algorithms optimized to execute inside microchips using milliwatts of power* — A subfield of machine learning focused on optimizing and executing neural network models directly on resource-constrained microcontrollers operating in the milliwatt or microwatt power envelope.
- [**UART**](../../concepts/uart/en.md): *two-wire hardware circuit that sends and receives data character by character without a shared clock; asynchronous serial communication module commonly used for terminal logs and debugging* — A physical integrated circuit hardware block that translates parallel computing data into sequential asynchronous serial bitstreams over paired transmit (TX) and receive (RX) wires.
- [**Wearable device**](../../concepts/wearable/en.md): *electronic gadget worn directly on the human body to monitor activity and health; body-worn tech like smartwatches, fitness rings, and biometric patches* — A category of compact electronic devices designed to be comfortably worn on the body or clothing, incorporating sensors, processing, and wireless transceivers for continuous biometric tracking.
- [**Wi-Fi**](../../concepts/wifi/en.md): *ubiquitous wireless radio technology that connects computers, phones, and IoT devices to the internet; high-speed wireless local area network standard operating over 2.4 GHz, 5 GHz, and 6 GHz bands* — A family of wireless network protocols based on the IEEE 802.11 standards used for local area networking of devices and high-speed internet access across licensed-exempt radio bands.
- [**Z-Wave**](../../concepts/z-wave/en.md): *sub-gigahertz wireless mesh protocol for smart homes that avoids crowded Wi-Fi channels; reliable home automation radio standard operating on dedicated sub-1GHz frequencies* — A wireless communications protocol operating in the sub-gigahertz frequency spectrum designed specifically for reliable, interference-free mesh networking in residential home automation.
- [**Zigbee**](../../concepts/zigbee/en.md): *low power wireless mesh networking standard commonly used in home automation systems; radio protocol that routes sensor messages across interconnected household devices* — A low-power, wireless mesh networking standard built upon IEEE 802.15.4, designed for multi-device home automation and smart energy management.
