- [**Zero trust**](../../concepts/zero-trust/en.md): *a security model that never assumes any user or device is safe, even inside the office network; the principle of verifying identity and device health for every single digital request* — A cybersecurity framework requiring continuous verification of every user, device, and network transaction, operating under the assumption that breaches are already present.
