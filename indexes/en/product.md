# Termbridge Index: Product and Project Management (en)

- [**A/B testing**](../../concepts/ab-testing/en.md): *splitting users between two variants of a screen or feature to see which one performs better; controlled trial where real users are randomly split to measure which design drives higher conversions* — A randomized experimental method where two variants of a digital experience are shown to visitors simultaneously to determine which drives superior outcomes.
- [**Acceptance criteria**](../../concepts/acceptance-criteria/en.md): *specific checklist of conditions that a user story must satisfy before being marked complete; unambiguous rules showing developers and testers exactly how a feature should behave* — A predefined set of conditions, behaviors, and boundary checks that a specific user story must satisfy to be accepted.
- [**Burndown chart**](../../concepts/burndown-chart/en.md): *visual graph showing remaining work decreasing day by day throughout a sprint; progress tracking chart comparing actual remaining effort against an ideal completion slope* — A graphical representation of work left to do versus time, utilized by agile squads to forecast sprint completion trajectories.
- [**Churn rate**](../../concepts/churn-rate/en.md): *percentage of customers who stop using or paying for a service during a given time period; rate at which subscribers cancel memberships or abandon an application* — The share of paying users who stop, by cancelling or not renewing, during a given period.
- [**Customer lifetime value (CLV)**](../../concepts/customer-lifetime-value/en.md): *total amount of money a business expects to earn from a customer throughout their entire relationship; projected net profit or revenue generated by a single user account over its lifespan* — The projected total revenue or profit that a business anticipates earning from a single customer throughout the duration of their relationship.
- [**Daily active users (DAU)**](../../concepts/daily-active-users/en.md): *count of unique people who open and interact with an application in a single day; vital metric tracking daily user volume and ongoing product engagement* — The total count of unique individuals who initiate a meaningful interaction with a software application within a 24-hour day.
- [**Daily Scrum**](../../concepts/daily-scrum/en.md): *quick fifteen-minute daily morning meeting for developers to sync on sprint progress; brief stand-up check-in where teammates share achievements, plans, and immediate blockers* — A 15-minute daily inspect-and-adapt event for developers to synchronize activities and plan the next 24 hours of work.
- [**Definition of Done (DoD)**](../../concepts/definition-of-done/en.md): *shared quality checklist that every piece of work must meet before being considered finished; formal agreement covering code reviews, automated tests, documentation, and deployment steps* — A formal commitment detailing the technical and quality standards that every product increment must satisfy to be release-ready.
- [**Epic (Agile)**](../../concepts/epic-agile/en.md): *large body of work that spans multiple sprints and is split into several user stories; major product initiative or feature theme organized across a longer development timeframe* — A substantial strategic initiative in agile software delivery that encompasses a collection of related user stories delivered over several sprints.
- [**Kanban**](../../concepts/kanban/en.md): *visual project management board that tracks work across columns and limits tasks in progress; continuous delivery system focused on smoothing workflow and eliminating production bottlenecks* — A visual workflow management method designed to optimize continuous flow, limit work in progress, and maximize efficiency.
- [**Key Performance Indicator (KPI)**](../../concepts/kpi/en.md): *measurable target tracking whether a company or team is meeting its core operational goals; key business metric showing the ongoing performance and financial health of a product* — A quantifiable metric tracking ongoing operational efficacy and business performance against strategic benchmarks.
- [**MoSCoW method**](../../concepts/moscow-method/en.md): *prioritization technique that sorts requirements into Must have, Should have, Could have, and Won't have; categorization method ensuring teams deliver essential deliverables first while managing scope expectations* — A qualitative prioritization technique sorting project requirements into four distinct categories: Must have, Should have, Could have, and Won't have.
- [**Minimum Viable Product (MVP)**](../../concepts/mvp/en.md): *simplest early version of a product built with just enough features to test with real users; functional initial release designed to validate core business hypotheses with minimal engineering effort* — An early iteration of a product incorporating the minimum feature set necessary to validate foundational assumptions with real customers.
- [**North Star metric**](../../concepts/north-star-metric/en.md): *single key metric that best captures the core value a product delivers to its customers; overarching guiding metric that aligns product, engineering, and marketing teams toward long-term growth* — The primary operational metric capturing the core value delivered to customers that serves as the leading indicator of sustainable business growth.
- [**Objectives and Key Results (OKR)**](../../concepts/okr/en.md): *goal-setting system that pairs an inspiring objective with measurable numerical results; quarterly framework aligning company direction, team focus, and quantifiable targets* — A collaborative goal-setting framework pairing ambitious qualitative objectives with measurable key results to track organizational progress.
- [**Product Requirements Document (PRD)**](../../concepts/prd/en.md): *comprehensive guide detailing what problem a feature solves, what it should do, and how success is measured; foundational specification shared between product managers, designers, and software engineers* — A definitive planning document detailing product purpose, target personas, functional requirements, and measurable success criteria for delivery teams.
- [**Product backlog**](../../concepts/product-backlog/en.md): *prioritized master list of features, bug fixes, and technical tasks waiting to be built; single authoritative repository of planned work for the entire software product team* — An evolving, ordered catalog of features, enhancements, and fixes representing the single source of work for a product team.
- [**Product discovery**](../../concepts/product-discovery/en.md): *research and experimentation process used to figure out what product is worth building before writing code; systematic validation of customer problems, usability, feasibility, and business viability* — The exploratory process of validating customer problems and potential product solutions to ensure engineering teams build valuable, viable offerings.
- [**Product-market fit**](../../concepts/product-market-fit/en.md): *stage where a product satisfies strong customer demand in a large and viable market; state where customers love a product, retention stabilizes, and growth happens organically* — The commercial milestone achieved when a product satisfies the demands of an attractive market, evidenced by sustained user retention and organic adoption.
- [**Product owner (PO)**](../../concepts/product-owner/en.md): *person responsible for deciding what features a product should have and setting their priority; key team member who connects user needs and business goals with development work* — The Scrum role accountable for maximizing product value and managing the prioritized content of the product backlog.
- [**Release (Product)**](../../concepts/product-release/en.md): *formal deployment and launch of a finished software version to end users; milestone package combining tested features, bug fixes, and documentation into production* — The formal deployment and distribution of a production-ready software version to customers alongside associated release notes and assets.
- [**Product roadmap**](../../concepts/product-roadmap/en.md): *high-level strategic timeline communicating where a product is heading and why; visual guide that aligns executives, developers, and customers on major product milestones* — A strategic, evolving visual document mapping the vision, overarching direction, and planned milestones of a product over time.
- [**Retention rate**](../../concepts/retention-rate/en.md): *percentage of users who continue using or paying for an app over a given timeframe; metric demonstrating how effectively a product keeps existing customers engaged over time* — The percentage of customers that a business retains over a specified duration, reflecting enduring product value and satisfaction.
- [**RICE scoring**](../../concepts/rice-scoring/en.md): *formula to rank features by multiplying reach, impact, and confidence, then dividing by effort; scoring model helping product managers objectively decide which projects deliver the highest return on investment* — A quantitative prioritization framework that scores potential initiatives by multiplying Reach, Impact, and Confidence and dividing by Effort.
- [**Scope creep**](../../concepts/scope-creep/en.md): *uncontrolled growth in project features and requirements without adding extra time or budget; gradual piling on of unplanned requests that derails software delivery schedules* — The gradual and uncontrolled expansion of project scope beyond initial goals without corresponding adjustments to schedule, budget, or resources.
- [**Scrum**](../../concepts/scrum/en.md): *collaborative framework for developing complex products in short timeboxed iterations; structured agile method featuring specific roles, backlogs, and iterative review meetings* — A lightweight process framework structured around transparency, inspection, and adaptation to build products incrementally.
- [**Scrum master**](../../concepts/scrum-master/en.md): *coach who guides the team in agile practices and removes blockers that slow down development; facilitator who protects engineers from external distractions and improves team collaboration* — The Scrum role accountable for establishing Scrum practices, promoting team effectiveness, and removing operational impediments.
- [**Sprint (Agile)**](../../concepts/sprint-agile/en.md): *fixed development timebox usually running two weeks to build working features; regular repeating cycle where a team converts selected backlog tasks into finished software* — A timeboxed iteration of one month or less during which a team builds a functional, verified product increment.
- [**Sprint planning**](../../concepts/sprint-planning/en.md): *collaborative meeting held at the start of a sprint to decide what work to tackle next; planning session where the team establishes a sprint goal and selects backlog items* — The collaborative event initiating each sprint where the Scrum team defines the sprint goal and forecasts backlog delivery.
- [**Sprint retrospective**](../../concepts/sprint-retrospective/en.md): *end-of-sprint team meeting to review what went well and identify concrete ways to improve; safe reflective space where teammates evaluate tools, processes, and interpersonal collaboration* — The final event of a sprint where the Scrum team inspects its past performance regarding people, relationships, process, and tools to identify actionable improvements.
- [**Stakeholder**](../../concepts/stakeholder/en.md): *anyone who has an interest in, influences, or is affected by the outcome of a product; key parties connected to a project, including end users, business leaders, investors, and regulatory bodies* — An individual, team, or organization that can affect, be affected by, or perceive itself to be affected by a product decision or outcome.
- [**Story point**](../../concepts/story-point/en.md): *abstract measurement unit used to estimate the relative effort, complexity, and risk of a software task; scoring system that rates user story difficulty using relative comparisons rather than calendar hours* — A relative number that agile teams use to estimate how much total effort a backlog item will take, compared with other items.
- [**User story**](../../concepts/user-story/en.md): *short informal description of a software feature framed from the perspective of an end user; practical work item explaining who needs a capability, what they need, and why it provides value* — A concise, non-technical explanation of a software requirement capturing user intent, desired functionality, and business value.
- [**Velocity (Agile)**](../../concepts/velocity-agile/en.md): *average amount of work a development team delivers during a single sprint; historical metric measuring how many story points a squad completes per iteration* — A planning metric tracking the average volume of story points or backlog items completed by a team per sprint.
