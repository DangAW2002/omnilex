# Homonyms Index (ja)

Terms sharing a label or alias, distinguished by context:

## LoRA
- [**Low-Rank Adaptation (AI)**](../../concepts/lora/en.md) [it/ai-ml/mlops]: A parameter-efficient fine-tuning method that freezes the original weights and trains small low-rank matrices that represent the weight update.
- [**LoRa (IoT)**](../../concepts/lora-radio/en.md) [iot/connectivity-protocols]: A proprietary physical-layer wireless radio frequency modulation technique derived from Chirp Spread Spectrum (CSS) that enables long-range, low-power data communications across license-free sub-gigahertz bands.

## Margin
- [**Margin (Trading)**](../../concepts/margin/en.md) [finance/investing/stocks]: Assets in a brokerage account support a loan used to buy securities.
- [**Profit margin (Accounting)**](../../concepts/profit-margin/en.md) [finance/corporate-finance/financial-statements]: A percentage comparing a specified measure of profit with the corresponding revenue.

## Lead
- [**Lead (Journalism)**](../../concepts/news-lead/en.md) [media/journalism/reporting]: The introductory sentence or paragraph of a news story that delivers the most essential facts immediately to the audience.
- [**Lead (Marketing)**](../../concepts/sales-lead/en.md) [media/digital-marketing/metrics-analytics]: An individual or organization that has expressed interest in a business's products or services and provided contact details for future communication.

## Thread
- [**Thread (IoT)**](../../concepts/thread-iot/en.md) [iot/connectivity-protocols]: An IPv6-based, low-power wireless mesh networking protocol built on IEEE 802.15.4 that provides reliable, secure, and self-healing connectivity for smart home devices without requiring a central hub.
- [**Thread (Programming)**](../../concepts/thread-programming/en.md) [it/software-engineering/programming-concepts]: The smallest unit of execution within an operating system process, sharing its parent memory space and resources with other threads.

## Token
- [**Token (Authentication)**](../../concepts/token-auth/en.md) [it/security/authentication, it/web/api]: A cryptographically signed string presented to servers to prove identity and authorization permissions.
- [**Token (Cryptocurrency)**](../../concepts/token-crypto/en.md) [finance/investing/digital-assets]: A digital unit of value issued on top of an existing smart contract blockchain platform.
- [**Token (AI)**](../../concepts/token-llm/en.md) [it/ai-ml/llm-agents]: Small chunk of text, typically subword units, processed by large language models.
