# Termbridge Index: Media (ja)

- [**Cookie**](cookie): *small browser file websites save to remember your login; web tracker banner you click accept on* — A small piece of data stored on the user's browser by websites to retain state and track browsing activity.
- [**Return on Investment**](roi): *how much money you make back compared to what you spent; performance metric measuring profitability of an expense* — A performance metric used to evaluate the efficiency or profitability of an investment relative to its cost.
