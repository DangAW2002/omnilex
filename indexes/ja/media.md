# Termbridge Index: Media (ja)

- [**A/B testing**](../../concepts/ab-testing/en.md): *splitting users between two variants of a screen or feature to see which one performs better; controlled trial where real users are randomly split to measure which design drives higher conversions* — A randomized experimental method where two variants of a digital experience are shown to visitors simultaneously to determine which drives superior outcomes.
- [**Impression (Ads)**](../../concepts/ad-impression/en.md): *every single time an advertisement appears on someone's screen; the total view count of an ad including repeat sightings by the same person* — A metric recording each individual occurrence of an advertisement being fetched and rendered on a user's viewport screen.
- [**Anonymous source**](../../concepts/anonymous-source/en.md): *an informant providing facts to a journalist without their identity being publicly disclosed; a protected whistleblower or insider quoted without their real name* — An informant who shares factual intelligence or testimony with a reporter under an agreement that their personal identity will remain confidential.
- [**Aspect ratio**](../../concepts/aspect-ratio/en.md): *the proportional relationship between the width and height of an image or screen; screen dimension proportion such as 16:9 widescreen or 9:16 vertical smartphone format* — The proportional ratio describing the relationship of an image, frame, or display device's width relative to its height.
- [**Reach (Marketing)**](../../concepts/audience-reach/en.md): *the number of unique individual people who see your content or ad; how many different people your message successfully touches* — The total number of distinct, unduplicated individuals or user accounts exposed to a piece of media content or advertisement during a specified period.
- [**B-roll**](../../concepts/b-roll/en.md): *secondary video footage cut into a scene to visually support the primary interview or narrative; background and illustrative camera shots used in film editing to cover cuts and enhance context* — Supplemental video footage spliced alongside primary interview or action shots to illustrate topics, hide edit splices, and enrich storytelling.
- [**Backlink**](../../concepts/backlink/en.md): *a clickable link on another website that points back to yours; an external vote of confidence connecting another page to your site* — An incoming hyperlink from an external web domain directing users and web crawlers to a target page on another website.
- [**Bounce rate**](../../concepts/bounce-rate/en.md): *the percentage of visitors who leave a website after viewing only one page; how often people immediately exit a site without interacting further* — A digital analytics metric quantifying the share of site visits lacking meaningful user engagement, where guests depart without triggering secondary actions.
- [**Breaking news**](../../concepts/breaking-news/en.md): *real-time reports on urgent events developing as we speak; immediate media updates about sudden occurrences of high public interest* — Newly received information about an urgent, ongoing event that warrants immediate public dissemination across news outlets.
- [**Byline**](../../concepts/byline/en.md): *the line crediting the reporter who investigated and wrote the piece; attribution stating who authored a printed or online article* — A line in a publication identifying the writer or contributor responsible for the reporting and prose of an article.
- [**Call to action (CTA)**](../../concepts/call-to-action/en.md): *a prompt or button telling a visitor exactly what step to take next; the direct invitation urging people to buy, sign up, or click now* — A marketing prompt, button, or statement designed to encourage immediate user response and guide prospects toward a desired action.
- [**Color grading**](../../concepts/color-grading/en.md): *stylized color adjustment enhancing emotional tone and artistic mood in videos and photographs; post-production color manipulation shaping the visual palette and atmosphere of footage* — The creative process in video post-production where color properties, tonal balances, and contrast curves are altered to produce an artistic mood or stylized look.
- [**Content marketing**](../../concepts/content-marketing/en.md): *creating helpful articles, videos, and guides to attract and keep customers; marketing by teaching and entertaining people rather than just pitching products* — A strategic marketing approach focused on creating and distributing valuable, relevant, and consistent content to attract and retain a clearly defined audience.
- [**Conversion rate**](../../concepts/conversion-rate/en.md): *the percentage of website visitors who complete a desired action like buying or signing up; how successfully your web traffic turns into actual paying customers or leads* — The percentage of total visitors or interactions that culminate in the completion of a defined goal or desired transaction.
- [**Cookie**](../../concepts/cookie/en.md): *small browser file websites save to remember your login; web tracker banner you click accept on* — A small piece of data stored on the user's browser by websites to retain state and track browsing activity.
- [**Copyright**](../../concepts/copyright/en.md): *the legal right giving creators exclusive control over how their works are copied and shared; intellectual property protection preventing unauthorized reproduction of original creative works* — A form of intellectual property law protecting original works of authorship fixed in any tangible medium of expression from unauthorized copying and distribution.
- [**Cost per click (CPC)**](../../concepts/cpc/en.md): *the amount of money you pay each time a person clicks your ad; total ad cost divided by the number of clicks received* — An online advertising pricing model where the advertiser pays a fee each time a user clicks on one of their ads.
- [**Cost per mille (CPM)**](../../concepts/cpm/en.md): *the price an advertiser pays for every one thousand times an ad is seen; ad cost calculated per batch of one thousand views* — An advertising pricing model where the buyer pays a predetermined rate for every one thousand impressions their ad receives.
- [**Creative Commons**](../../concepts/creative-commons/en.md): *standardized public licenses enabling creators to share works with flexible permissions; open copyright framework allowing free cultural reuse while reserving specific conditions* — A nonprofit organization and legal framework providing standardized copyright licenses that permit free public distribution and remixing of creative works under specified conditions.
- [**Crisis communication**](../../concepts/crisis-communication/en.md): *messaging strategies used to protect reputation during unexpected emergencies; handling media and public briefings when an institution faces severe operational failure* — Strategic public communications deployed during emergencies to mitigate reputational damage, protect public safety, and convey truthful instructions.
- [**Click-through rate (CTR)**](../../concepts/ctr/en.md): *the percentage of people who click on your link after seeing it; clicks divided by impressions expressed as a percentage* — Clicks on an ad or link as a share of the times it was shown.
- [**Click-through rate (CTR)**](../../concepts/ctr/en.md): *the percentage of people who click on your link after seeing it; clicks divided by impressions expressed as a percentage* — Clicks on an ad or link as a share of the times it was shown.
- [**Open rate (Email)**](../../concepts/email-open-rate/en.md): *the percentage of delivered emails that recipients actually open to read; how many people out of your subscriber list clicked to open your message* — The percentage of successfully delivered emails opened by subscribers, indicating subject line effectiveness and audience engagement.
- [**Engagement rate**](../../concepts/engagement-rate/en.md): *the percentage of people who actively react, comment, or interact with your content; how enthusiastically an audience participates rather than just passively watching* — A performance metric that quantifies the level of active user interaction generated by content relative to its total reach, impressions, or followers.
- [**Fact-checking**](../../concepts/fact-checking/en.md): *verifying that claims, quotes, and statistics match verifiable evidence; investigating whether statements made by politicians and public figures are truthful* — The disciplined verification process of assessing factual assertions in published text, speeches, or broadcast reports against reliable primary documentation.
- [**Fair use**](../../concepts/fair-use/en.md): *legal exception allowing limited quotation of copyrighted material for review or teaching; rule permitting unlicensed use of creative works for criticism, parody, or news reporting* — A legal doctrine permitting limited quotation and borrowing of copyrighted works without permission for purposes such as criticism, news reporting, teaching, or scholarship.
- [**Hashtag**](../../concepts/hashtag/en.md): *a word or phrase beginning with a hash sign used to group topics; a clickable topic label on social networks that connects related posts* — A metadata tag prefixed by the pound or hash character used on social platforms and websites to categorize content and facilitate search.
- [**Headline**](../../concepts/headline/en.md): *the prominent title placed at the start of a news story; large text that tells readers what an article is about* — The prominent heading positioned above a news article that encapsulates its core subject and catches the reader's attention.
- [**Influencer marketing**](../../concepts/influencer-marketing/en.md): *partnering with popular creators to promote products to their followers; paying social media figures to recommend brands to their audience* — A form of social media marketing involving brand endorsements and product placements from creators with dedicated and engaged followings.
- [**Inverted pyramid**](../../concepts/inverted-pyramid/en.md): *news writing structure putting the most vital details right at the start; narrative framework where facts decrease in significance toward the end* — A storytelling framework in journalism where the most vital facts appear at the top, followed by supporting details in declining order of importance.
- [**Landing page**](../../concepts/landing-page/en.md): *the first web page you see right after clicking an advertisement; a dedicated page built to get visitors to take one specific action* — A standalone web page created specifically for marketing campaigns where visitors arrive after clicking an ad, email, or promotional link.
- [**Marketing funnel**](../../concepts/marketing-funnel/en.md): *the step-by-step journey a person takes from first hearing about a brand to buying; a model showing how a large crowd of potential customers narrows down to a few buyers* — A strategic framework illustrating the progressive stages of a customer's journey, from initial brand awareness through consideration to final purchase and retention.
- [**Lead (Journalism)**](../../concepts/news-lead/en.md): *the opening paragraph of a news article answering key questions; the first few sentences delivering the most crucial facts* — The introductory sentence or paragraph of a news story that delivers the most essential facts immediately to the audience.
- [**Newsletter**](../../concepts/newsletter/en.md): *a regularly sent email sharing news, articles, or updates with subscribers; an informative periodic email delivered directly to your inbox from a business or writer* — A regularly distributed direct-to-inbox electronic publication focused on sharing news, curated insights, and company updates with an opted-in subscriber base.
- [**Op-ed**](../../concepts/op-ed/en.md): *an opinion essay written by an outside contributor or guest author; a signed article offering reasoned personal perspectives on current issues* — An opinion piece published in a newspaper or journal reflecting the specific viewpoint of a named author unaffiliated with the publication's editorial board.
- [**Organic traffic**](../../concepts/organic-traffic/en.md): *visitors who find and click your website through unpaid search results; the free visitor stream arriving at your site from search engines* — Visitors who arrive at a website by clicking on unpaid search engine results rather than clicking paid advertisements.
- [**Paywall**](../../concepts/paywall/en.md): *digital barrier restricting full access to online news articles to paying subscribers; a website system requiring a payment or membership before viewing restricted media content* — A digital mechanism on online media websites restricting full access to premium articles and content behind paid subscriptions or memberships.
- [**Podcast**](../../concepts/podcast/en.md): *episodic digital audio program syndicated online for on-demand streaming and listening; spoken-word audio show subscribed to and played through streaming applications* — An episodic series of spoken-word digital audio files distributed over the internet that users can stream, download, and subscribe to automatically via feeds.
- [**Press conference**](../../concepts/press-conference/en.md): *a media briefing where reporters ask questions directly to organizational representatives; an organized gathering where public figures deliver statements and take press queries* — An organized event where an institution invites reporters to hear an announcement and ask questions on the record.
- [**Embargo (Media)**](../../concepts/press-embargo/en.md): *an advance agreement not to publish given information until a specified date and time; a temporary ban on releasing a news announcement before a set deadline* — A temporary restriction requested by an information source requiring media outlets to refrain from publishing shared material until a stipulated timestamp.
- [**Press release**](../../concepts/press-release/en.md): *an official written statement distributed by an organization to journalists; a prepared announcement presenting news from a company, government agency, or institution* — A formal written communication distributed to news organizations to deliver official announcements and generate accurate news coverage.
- [**Retargeting**](../../concepts/retargeting/en.md): *displaying ads to past visitors who left your website without purchasing; reminding prior visitors about products they already viewed online* — An online advertising technique that serves targeted ads to users who previously visited a website or app as they browse elsewhere on the web.
- [**Return on Investment**](../../concepts/roi/en.md): *how much money you make back compared to what you spent; performance metric measuring profitability of an expense* — A performance metric used to evaluate the efficiency or profitability of an investment relative to its cost.
- [**Lead (Marketing)**](../../concepts/sales-lead/en.md): *a prospective customer who has shared contact information or shown interest in your product; someone who might buy from your business in the near future* — An individual or organization that has expressed interest in a business's products or services and provided contact details for future communication.
- [**Search Engine Optimization (SEO)**](../../concepts/seo/en.md): *making a website appear higher on Google search results; getting free visitors to your web pages from search engines* — The practice of improving a website's technical setup and content relevance so search engines rank its pages higher in unpaid results.
- [**Keyword (SEO)**](../../concepts/seo-keyword/en.md): *the word or phrase someone types into Google when searching; the search term a web page targets to get ranked* — A specific word or phrase entered into search engines that webmasters target to connect user search queries with relevant content.
- [**Search Engine Results Page (SERP)**](../../concepts/serp/en.md): *the list of web links Google shows after you search; the results screen displaying answers and links for your query* — The web page returned by a search engine in response to a user's search query, displaying organic listings, paid ads, and rich visual elements.
- [**Spokesperson**](../../concepts/spokesperson/en.md): *the designated individual authorized to speak on behalf of an entity; an official representative delivering public statements for an organization* — An official designated to speak on behalf of a government body, corporation, or group, presenting its positions and answering queries.
- [**Storyboard**](../../concepts/storyboard/en.md): *visual sequence of illustrated panels sketching camera angles and action for a video shoot; drawn comic-strip plan showing how a commercial, animation, or movie scene will unfold* — A sequential visual layout of illustrations and annotations representing proposed camera angles, character movements, and staging before production begins.
- [**Typography**](../../concepts/typography/en.md): *the craft of arranging fonts and text layout to make printed or digital words legible and appealing; artistic styling and formatting of letterforms across page and screen compositions* — The art, craft, and technique of selecting and arranging typefaces, font weights, kerning, and spacing to render written language legible, readable, and visually engaging.
- [**UTM parameters**](../../concepts/utm-parameters/en.md): *extra text tags added to the end of a web link to track where visitors came from; snippets of code in a URL showing which specific ad or post generated a click* — Five standardized query parameters appended to URLs that allow analytics platforms to track the source, medium, and campaign details of inbound web traffic.
- [**Visual hierarchy**](../../concepts/visual-hierarchy/en.md): *the layout principle organizing design elements in order of visual importance; arranging scale, color, and placement so viewers intuitively spot the most crucial information first* — The strategic arrangement and presentation of graphic design elements in deliberate order of importance, guiding the viewer's eye across the layout seamlessly.
- [**Voice-over**](../../concepts/voice-over/en.md): *off-screen spoken audio narrating or explaining visual actions in a video or documentary; vocal recording heard by viewers where the speaker does not appear directly on screen* — A production technique where an unseen narrator's spoken commentary is recorded and played over accompanying visual material or broadcast segments.
